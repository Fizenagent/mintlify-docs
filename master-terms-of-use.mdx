## 3. Custodial vs Non-Custodial Services

<Info>
  The Platform offers some Services in which you retain self-custody of your Digital Assets, and other Services in which Fizen holds the relevant Digital Assets against an internal balance for you. The distinction is important for understanding your rights and risks.
</Info>

### 3.1 Non-custodial Services (you retain self-custody)

- **Fizen Wallet** — your Private Key is generated on your device and is never transmitted to or stored by Fizen. Fizen has no ability to access, freeze or move Digital Assets in your Fizen Wallet.
- **Fizen Swap** — Swaps are executed on-chain by independent decentralised exchange aggregators against transactions you sign with your own Private Key. Fizen does not hold the Digital Assets in transit.
- **Fizen Marketplace** — although you pay in crypto and Fizen converts to fiat to procure the Marketplace Item from the Supplier, your payment to Fizen is a one-off transaction in respect of a specific Order; Fizen does not maintain a balance for you in the Marketplace.
- **Fizen Tokenized Securities** — tokenized securities trades are executed on-chain through third-party aggregators and settle to your own Fizen Wallet. Fizen does not custody the tokenized securities.
- **Fizen Pay (for merchants)** — the merchant's settlement smart contract is owned by the merchant's own blockchain wallet. Fizen does not own or control the contract or the wallet.
- **Fizen on/off-ramp via Transak** — Transak is the counterparty for the fiat leg. Fizen does not hold fiat at any point.

### 3.2 Custodial-balance Services (Fizen holds a balance for you)

- **Fizen Card** — you deposit USDT to a Fizen-controlled wallet; Fizen pools deposits and tracks your individual balance in an internal database; Fizen prefunds the Card partner from the pool. Your Card balance is a personal claim against Fizen, not a self-custodied asset. See the Fizen Card Terms for full detail.
- **Fizen QR Pay** — same mechanic — deposits to a Fizen-controlled smart contract, balance tracked at Fizen, prefund to Local Payment Partner. See the Fizen QR Pay Terms for full detail.

### 3.3 Implications of the custodial-balance Services

If you use Fizen Card or Fizen QR Pay, you should understand that:

- Your balance is not a bank deposit and is not insured by any deposit-protection scheme;
- Your balance is a personal claim against Fizen (a creditor relationship);
- If Fizen becomes insolvent, has its wallets compromised, or is otherwise unable to honour its obligations, you may lose some or all of the balance;
- Fizen has the technical ability — and where required by Applicable Law, the obligation — to freeze, block or refuse to process Deposits, Withdrawals or transactions in respect of those balances;
- The full detail and risk allocation is in the Fizen Card Terms and the Fizen QR Pay Terms; please read them carefully before depositing.
