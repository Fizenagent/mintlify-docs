These Fizen Promotions Master Terms (the “Promotions Master Terms”) set out the framework applicable to all promotional campaigns Fizen offers (each, a “Campaign”). Each Campaign is also subject to its own Campaign Rules, which specify the period, eligibility, qualifying activity, reward and other Campaign-specific details. The Promotions Master Terms supplement, and form part of, the Fizen Master Terms of Use, and operate together with the Fizen Rewards Program Terms in respect of FiPoint-denominated rewards.

## **2. General Eligibility**

To participate in any Campaign, you must satisfy all eligibility requirements in clause 5 of the Fizen Master Terms of Use, hold an active Fizen account in good standing with KYC at the level required, be a natural person (unless a Campaign expressly states otherwise), be habitually resident in a jurisdiction in which the Campaign is offered, and satisfy any additional eligibility criteria in the Campaign Rules. Fizen employees, contractors and their immediate family members are not eligible unless expressly stated.

## **3. Participation, Validation and Reward Delivery**

Performing a Qualifying Activity makes you provisionally eligible for the Reward. Fizen validates and delivers the Reward within the time stated in the Campaign Rules. Fizen may delay or withhold pending validation, KYC re-verification, AML/sanctions checks, or fraud investigation.
