## 1. Definitions

In these QR Pay Terms:

- **Accepted Funding Transfer** means a Funding Transfer that has received the required Blockchain confirmations, passed Fizen’s applicable compliance and risk checks, and been accepted and credited by Fizen.
- **Available QR Pay Balance** means the portion of your QR Pay Balance that is not reserved, restricted, subject to a hold, or otherwise unavailable for Payments or Withdrawal.
- **Conversion Rate** means the exchange rate used to calculate the amount of QR Pay Balance required for a Payment, refund or other transaction, including any disclosed spread or conversion charge.
- **Funding Asset** means a Digital Asset that Fizen accepts from time to time to fund QR Pay. As at the effective date of these QR Pay Terms, the Funding Asset is USDT on the network or networks shown in the app.
- **Funding Transfer** means a transfer of a Funding Asset by you to a Blockchain address or smart contract designated by Fizen for funding QR Pay.
- **Home Jurisdiction** means the jurisdiction in which you are habitually resident, as reasonably determined by Fizen using your KYC information and other relevant information available to Fizen.
- **Local Payment Partner** means the bank, payment institution, payment intermediary or other provider that supports conversion, payment processing or local fiat settlement for QR Pay in a particular market. - **Merchant** means a person or business that can receive a Payment through a supported local or interoperable QR payment scheme. A Merchant does not need to have a direct contractual relationship with Fizen.
- **Merchant Jurisdiction** means the jurisdiction in which the Merchant receives the Payment.
- **Payment** means a transaction initiated by you through QR Pay to pay a Merchant.
- **QR Pay** means the QR-based payment service made available by Fizen through the Fizen Platform.
- **QR Pay Balance** means the amount displayed in the app as available or attributable to you for QR Pay. Legally, it represents Fizen’s unsecured contractual obligation to apply the relevant amount toward eligible Payments or, where available, satisfy it through Withdrawal. It does not represent assets held for you.
- **QR Pay Unit** means the unit in which your QR Pay Balance is denominated and displayed. At the effective date of these QR Pay Terms, the QR Pay Unit is USDT.
- **Reserved Balance** means the portion of your QR Pay Balance temporarily set aside after you confirm a Payment and before that Payment settles, fails, expires or is cancelled.
- **Settlement** or **Settled** means confirmation through the relevant Local Payment Partner or payment scheme that the Merchant payment has been successfully and finally processed, subject to any later refund, reversal or correction permitted by Applicable Law or scheme rules.
- **Withdrawal** means Fizen’s satisfaction of all or part of its QR Pay Balance obligation by transferring the corresponding Funding Asset to a supported Blockchain address in accordance with these QR Pay Terms.

### 6.2 Authorisation and reservation

When you confirm a Payment, you:

- authorise Fizen to reserve the displayed amount from your Available QR Pay Balance;
- instruct Fizen to request the relevant Local Payment Partner to process and settle the Payment; and
- confirm that you intend to pay the identified Merchant for the relevant goods or services.

Confirmation does not itself settle the Payment or extinguish Fizen’s obligation. The relevant amount becomes Reserved Balance while processing is pending and cannot be used for another Payment or Withdrawal.

### 6.3 Settlement and discharge

When Fizen receives confirmation that the Payment has Settled:

- the corresponding Reserved Balance is deducted from your QR Pay Balance; and
- Fizen’s contractual obligation to you is discharged and extinguished to the same extent.

The Merchant receives local fiat through the Local Payment Partner or supported QR scheme. The Merchant does not receive your original Funding Asset from you.

The release may not be immediate because Fizen may need confirmation from the Local Payment Partner or payment scheme. Fizen will not extinguish the relevant portion of its obligation merely because you attempted or confirmed a Payment that did not Settle.

If only part of a reserved amount Settles, Fizen will deduct the Settled amount and release the remainder. Fizen will not increase the amount of a Payment above the amount you authorised without obtaining any further authorisation required by Applicable Law.

You should not maintain a QR Pay Balance greater than you reasonably expect to use, and you should assess whether these risks are appropriate for you.
