## How to add your card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fizen Super App → Crypto Card">
    Go to the **Crypto Card** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add to Google Pay / Apple Pay">
    Follow the in-app steps to add your card to your phone wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done — tap to pay anywhere">
    Your Fizen Card is now available in Google Pay or Apple Pay. Tap your phone at any Visa terminal to pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>
