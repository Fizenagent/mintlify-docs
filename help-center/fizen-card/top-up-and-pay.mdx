## How to top up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fizen Super App → Crypto Card">
    Go to the **Crypto Card** section in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your top-up method">
    **From your QR Pay account** — transfer funds directly from your QR Pay balance to your Card account.

    **From your Fizen Wallet** — deposit USDT or USDC from your non-custodial Fizen Wallet (you'll need native token for gas fee).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and confirm">
    Your card balance updates instantly or within a few minutes depending on network conditions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to pay

Once topped up, use your Fizen Card like any Visa card:

- **Online** — enter card number, expiry, CVV at checkout
- **In-store** — tap to pay (NFC), chip & PIN, or swipe
- **Google Pay / Apple Pay** — [add your card](/help-center/fizen-card/add-card-to-google-pay-apple-pay) and tap your phone

Your USDT balance is automatically converted to the local currency at real-time FX rates.
