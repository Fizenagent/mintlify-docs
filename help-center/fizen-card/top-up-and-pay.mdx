### Top up

#### **Top-up from your QR Pay account**

Fizen is a crypto payment super app, so we also support QR Pay with USDt, such as Vietnam, the Philippines, Brazil, Georgia, Argentina, and more, coming soon. If you already have a QR Pay balance, you can transfer funds from your QR Pay account to your Card account

1. Choose Transfer from QR Pay account
2. Input your amount and confirm

#### **Top-up from your non-custodial Fizen Wallet**

1. Prepare funds in your non-custodial Fizen Wallet (please note that you need native token to pay gas fee)
2. Choose Deposit from your Fizen Wallet
3. Input your amount and confirm
4. Congrats, your card is ready to spend

**Supported Assets**

* USDT, USDC (Solana network)
* More networks are added regularly

#### **Top Up FAQs**

<details>

<summary>Is there a delay with top up?</summary>

Usually instant. Some networks require 3–12 confirmations, which might take few minutes

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I withdraw money from the card balance?</summary>

Yes, you can withdraw to your Fizen Wallet or your QR Pay account anytime.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is there a top-up fee? </summary>

Yes. There is a 0.5% top-up fee, but you enjoy a 0-fee top-up with your card/plan limit

</details>
