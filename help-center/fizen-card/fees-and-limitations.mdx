## Fees

| Fee | Standard | Applicable Now |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual Card Issuance | $9.99 | Free for pre-registered users |
| Monthly Maintenance (active card) | $0 if spending ≥ $500 / $1.50 if under | Free (first month) |
| FX Fee (non-USD payments) | 1% | 1% |
| Crypto → Fiat Conversion | 0.5% | 0.5% |
| Small transaction fee (under $50) | $0.50 | $0.50 |
| Close Card Fee | $2.00 | $2.00 |
| ATM Withdrawal (physical card) | $2 + 1.5% | Free first withdrawal up to $200/month |

<Note>
  Some fees may be waived based on your [card benefits](/help-center/fizen-card/benefits).
</Note>
