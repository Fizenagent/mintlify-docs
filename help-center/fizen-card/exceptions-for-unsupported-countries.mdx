If you are a citizen of a [restricted country](/help-center/fizen-card/unsupported-countries) but **currently live in a supported country**, you may still be eligible for a Fizen Card.

---

## How it works

During KYC, select your **country of residency** (not your home country) and submit valid proof of residency.

**Accepted documents:**
- Residence permit / resident card
- Eligible long-term visa (where permitted)
- Local driver's license issued by your country of residence
- Other official government-issued residency documents (case-by-case)

Approval is subject to compliance checks. Fizen reserves the right to request additional documents or decline applications based on regulatory requirements.

---

## Step-by-step

Follow the standard card application process — just make sure to select your **country of residence** during verification, not your home country.
