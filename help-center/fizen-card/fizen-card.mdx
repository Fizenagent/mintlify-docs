## Why Fizen Card

- **Spend instantly** — Tap to Pay, Chip & PIN, Magstripe, QR Scan. Works like any Visa card
- **Simple flow** — Load USDT, spend in USD or local currency
- **Real-time FX** — Spend in local currency wherever you are
- **Up to 200 cards** per account
- **Google Pay & Apple Pay** supported
- **No holds. No freezes** — No bank can freeze it. What you load, you can spend
- **Restricted countries welcome** — Russian and Chinese nationals with eligible documents can apply
- **24/7 instant human support**

---

## This card is for you if

- You hold USDT and you're tired of paying 2–5% to spend it via P2P
- You travel around SEA and want an all-in-one payment solution
- You run ad spend on Meta, Google, or TikTok
- You're a freelancer paid in USDT and done losing money on every conversion
- You're from a restricted country and every fintech door has been closed

<Note>
  Backed by **Tether** — the company behind the USDT in your wallet.
</Note>
