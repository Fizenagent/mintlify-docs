## Why Fizen Card?

* Spend instantly: Tap to Pay, Chip & PIN, Magstripe, QR Scan. Works like any Visa card.

* Real-time FX: Spend in local currency wherever you are.

* Restricted countries accepted: Russian and Chinese nationals with eligible documents can apply.
* 24/7 instant human support&#x20;
* Top-grade security: No crypto exposure on spend. Stablecoin backing.
