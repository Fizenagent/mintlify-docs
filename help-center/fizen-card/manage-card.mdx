## What you can manage

From the **Crypto Card** section in the Fizen app:

- **Card details** — view card number, expiry, and CVV
- **Freeze / Unfreeze** — instantly lock your card if lost or not in use
- **Transaction history** — full record of every spend
- **Balance** — view current USDT balance on your card
- **Multiple cards** — manage up to 200 cards from one account
