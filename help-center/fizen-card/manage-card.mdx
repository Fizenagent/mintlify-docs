# Manage Card

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Adventure Card Virtual FRONT (1).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

<a href="https://url.fizen.io/downloadgb" class="button primary" data-icon="credit-card">Get your card</a>

Once your card is set up successfully, it’s ready to serve you. You can use Fizen card for both real-life payment and online purchase, anywhere Visa is accepted. Also, you can add the card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
You can learn more about adding a card to Apple Pay [here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204506) and Google Pay [here](https://support.google.com/wallet/answer/12058983?hl=en).
{% endhint %}

After setting up your card, you can easily manage it through your Card account, including:

* Check the Card details
* Freeze/Unfreeze card
* View transaction history
* View the tokens’ balance in your card
