---
title: "Filter invoices"
---


On the [Invoices ](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/invoices)page, use the Status filter to view invoices according to their status.&#x20;

Fizen Pay gives you the option to find your invoices by customer email and invoice ID. You can use the search bar together with the filter to filter your invoices in more detail.
