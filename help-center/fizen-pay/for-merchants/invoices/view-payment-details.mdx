---
title: "View payment details"
---


If an invoice has been paid, you can see its payment detail&#x20;

**Step 1**. From the Invoices page, select the invoice you want to see details.

Click on the **View payment details** button.

**Step 2**. On the **Payment details** page, you can see the details of the payment for your invoice.
