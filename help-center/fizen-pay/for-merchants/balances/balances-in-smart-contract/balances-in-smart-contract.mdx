---
title: "Balances in Smart Contract"
---


When a customer pays you, the funds will first go to your Smart Contracts.&#x20;

\- The total amount displayed on the top is the total payable amount you will receive. Fizen Pay has already deducted your service fee.

<figure><img src="https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/tVS6Yr92rd2WTl49Hy4M9vzACfM82kjOKp7cEnrwb874VsXIDKIRwJkD4SQ9RUiC26qxYuWl5DhkBiwYWAuUY6eZdXmavpoeFFfso0A-EgLHQRkvXqONBKVn6wnM3dyWTtdvm6db5JgmWe2b04qCUd4" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

\- The list of cryptocurrencies is the cryptocurrencies you accepted in [Crypto settings](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/crypto-settings) page, or the cryptocurrencies you used to accept in the past and have not withdrawn funds to your wallet yet.

<figure><img src="https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/FVal434zKbgsMd1J1osCO30y0GUeGuxDdDRH56xqneUfsuAsuLX76NbDMVIJS2bbPYWwdQ7m2xfEzQbZoVLoiuSbonlH9kdvsK14Y73hHZJnOgMUnWn1Ti5Sv44C1WdAddLAuHGVlkXdLxdVN0N2xrY" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

\- You can check the balance of each cryptocurrency and see their values when converted to your local currency. You can change your local currency in [Account settings](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/settings) > **Payment settings** > **Local currency**.

<figure><img src="https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/OqvtQP4pn3qL6AOwsDdVhJkAg2OUHPmaU2KDJyoGqVDflujfLVcOAtJam3iKUxGrEe5FgLOszoAWlixFZ1JTmZC7oSfogsrZnh1fUfscdTsospcnIWked0H-8-B5dVPcu1fkMU7Z0ouQlT70TK6V63E" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

\- You can set your withdrawal schedule in [Account settings](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/settings). Your withdrawal schedule refers to how often Fizen Pay withdraws money from your smart contract to your wallet.

See more: [Set withdrawal schedule with auto-withdrawals](../../account-settings/auto-withdrawals.md)

If you choose to withdraw manually, you can do it in the Balances page.
