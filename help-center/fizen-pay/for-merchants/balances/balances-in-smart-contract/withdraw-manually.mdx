## 1. Click on the Withdraw button next to the cryptocurrency you want to withdraw

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/WUlHYJEvzoqg163Lw4FRpR17mTadcHDe8SFS6Zvt8Ue083g88Zp3KSwdvr8IZTa7gY0DZcaydV6aZ2nHxulaD8o_e0GFdUUOHCqf2uvFKFhGNlIcaHstMe57cx-zMiNeOPflwnzg5GzmKa-lbApNFEc)

The **Withdraw** button will be disabled if:

* The balance of the cryptocurrency is 0 (which means you have nothing to withdraw)

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/YoBmL2P7FszQUJqHeDOlXsoNfaMtg9SG-lCEBlqlIDYsNaAzgbIumjdA0SC48kjTG_ChPYl8BHukkrAWlIDVm912gk79zRTNZJw0Iw7_3XYMPJgwQj8cXQxD0pdT-ELnVqpfJlS0SFEieuSvVmW0C4A)

* The balance of the cryptocurrency is from payments whose status is UNDERPAID, OVERPAID, DELAYED, MULTIPLE, or OTHER.

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/pX8PFi9ior594iBCLr7X2hDpLCqDbBotIPYIVflUFNPH9ub09OZ3-y8k6b0HbtHEltcDTvUIkthjITlDTaq7buF_-V8Lx_IQzoCmEZ2VqZ2nJwNK77xNdz0N5OBApuJ21j8o8_UUYSI51Y6BDDDbhTE)

You have to mark these payments as resolved or refunded in order to withdraw their funds.

See more: [How to mark payments as resolved](../../payments/mark-a-payment-as-resolved.md)

See more: [How to mark payments as refunded](../../payments/mark-a-payment-as-refunded.md)


If the **Withdraw** button is enabled, a confirmation modal will appear.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/NvwqiM5T_t3R8utay9FhTUnnboNpGNjLzQtXfHiEocP06UqBhsmceSnvdeKBx_6TU7JMTFOMK3hWYsjRa0w7im2gjezE_I_ET9AQDXleeaDdKn7b2pFERRBEawSm9JYlMgCCuA2H7Ld8nciGn0akjpw)

**Explaination of the image:**

| Terms                       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blockchain network          | The blockchain network of the crypto currency you are withdrawing                                                                                                                                                                |
| Receiving wallet address    | The address of the hot wallet you created when you set up your account. Your cryptocurrencies will be withdrawn to this wallet                                                                                                   |
| Amount to be withdrawn )    | The amount you can withdraw, which includes received payments whose status is COMPLETED, RESOLVED, or REFUNDED. The received payments whose status is UNDERPAID, OVERPAID, MULTIPLE, or DELAYED are not included in this amount. |
| Est. gas fee                | The estimated network gas fee needed to execute the withdrawal transaction. 
See more: [Why do I have to pay a network fee to withdraw and how much is this fee?](../../../../../)
                       |
| Est. amount to be received  | Equals Amount to be withdrawn minus Est. gas fee.                                                                                                                                                                                |

The actual amount received in your wallet may differ a little from the **Est. amount to be received** due to the volatility of the gas fee.

If the **Est. Amount to be received** is negative, meaning the Amount to be withdrawn is less than the **Est. gas fee** to withdraw it, then you cannot withdraw.

See more:[ Is there a minimum amount that I need to withdraw?](https://pay-faqs.fizen.io/for-merchants/withdrawals#is-there-a-minimum-amount-that-i-need-to-withdraw)

See more: [Is there a maximum amount that I can withdraw?](https://pay-faqs.fizen.io/for-merchants/withdrawals#is-there-a-maximum-amount-that-i-can-withdraw)


After you click **Confirm**, the withdrawal process will begin. Normally you will receive the funds in your wallet after a few seconds to a few minutes.
