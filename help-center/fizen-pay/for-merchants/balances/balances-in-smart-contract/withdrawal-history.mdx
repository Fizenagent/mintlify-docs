**Explaination of status**

|  Status                 | Description                                                            | Possible actions                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Success                 | The funds are successfully sent to your crypto wallet.                 | No action needed. You can view the transaction hash on the blockchain by clicking the **View transaction** button |
| Failure                 | Transaction failed and the funds are still in your smart contract.     | You can try withdrawing them again.                                                                               |
| Transaction submitting  | Transaction is being sent to the blockchain to be included in a block. | No action needed. You wait for its status to change to **Blockchain confirming**.                                 |
| Blockchain confirming   | The blockchain is validating your transaction                          | No action needed. You wait for its status to change to **Success** or **Failure**                                 |
