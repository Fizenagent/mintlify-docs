---
title: "Create a POS"
---


Before you start to create your first POS, please decide which crypto currency you want to receive for your invoice by going to [Crypto settings](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/crypto-settings) page.&#x20;

[Learn more about how to use the crypto settings page.](../crypto-settings.md)

After selecting your accepted crypto currencies, you can start creating your POS.



**Step 1**. In the [Points of sale](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/pos) page, click on the Create new POS button on the right.

<figure><img src="https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/4bkqPJ1qsC8tuTiyDTGvnJSXFbIXvdc5R3bRgRVx-2kgwguFRJGNuPjBouBnfOm09Pq66IVbURmxX9jpS8MW3K7kjI_tCNK_UfEFnbfoV86GVbivboS9VqCK1Wo2zpdLDTYnHczZljlDt3cMA9Oame4" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2**. Input your POS information:

\-  **POS page header**

\- **Logo**

\- **Currency type**: If you choose Local currency, the your price will be listed as your local currency (eg. USD), and your customers can pay with any of the cryptos you set in [Crypto settings](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/crypto-settings) page. If you choose Crypto currency, your price will be listed as the crypto currency you choose, and your customers can pay with that crypto currency only.

<figure><img src="https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/2G86OyNwMxXFt6tblaoM7x568PeNwMxIoChqF1PxpVlGXSFzcAUgL_idd-hLZu4uXlCrXFkjr3K5Mjd_gI-7HH0Cr9Ksqqh-FX1xAOUDXtzcXt5jnwRVp8HYGAdLoDgC5a0YH7gKQULgKCnP6PNx404" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**. Click **Save**&#x20;

To get the QR code for your newly created POS, click on the QR icon next to it.

<figure><img src="https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/CzzZ5AwHsOym3IslOwS8I8rw6sWhXgzbz-_6ACoVbaoBD3_0KV8xH6Ijff_HW9vPFWLkFVc_vCcl00QQVbrNIQSBkNfEJT-_EuxquQgmrdNUwVmeW7W7DpdVJqsHlgH-xcIVgDL1bVLlY_6aVILAaQY" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can take a screenshot or download it as PNG file, print it out and stick it in front of your physical store for your customers to pay.
