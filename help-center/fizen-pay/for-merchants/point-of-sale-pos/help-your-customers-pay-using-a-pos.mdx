---
title: "Help your customers pay using a POS&#x20;"
---


When you scan the QR code, you will go to the POS page.

**Step 1**. Input the payment amount, click **Pay**. The system will generate a payment page for you with that amount.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/XZtTm89lvz8y3V9hUH7iCp62iVQFoHaskVULGAgrXIY4fBJsxR89LHjXe5Kh2gpRFShccWUk5k4qF1-c2Kf_MmvWLDQIDDv-iCqLP7W_AXpBYQYlIReG6NYRSMVOQJL3RXVFvh12KoGWkt5tKIM-kds)

**Step 2**. You can send that payment page to your customer by choosing **Switch device**

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/2fZJmVLjKQuh85FdJ8tpKJ4Tziw8rQcaTDaN9Heqs-v6YhP4SHeAqgUa5pdUP_4vRcw2ka7DCSY0b3doH5rK_tu5svkWGnEuhzQZktqFm2BaGOLg8y13zxukxhfwCwKMMr9gxEp-eTsvj9xJGDgCrO4)

**Step 3**. Let them scan the QR code and complete the payment.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3XvPc7-L7h9Q_m9tOg7b7C_5U4fAr74iOip5tMdzpjuYvTGTZ6fuKLB0YG-_bIWPriy09ypMoVl8VZXN6zKrIQ0fnYpeNQUeAXCDGYkQWu_BlnC-WnCKLPO8Wz8g90VryJtM5-y9ubCsNmJ3P6-YyOo)

Of course, your customer can scan the initial QR code and input the amount themselves, that’s okay too&#x20;
