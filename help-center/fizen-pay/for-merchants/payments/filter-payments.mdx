---
title: "Filter payments"
---


On the [Payments ](https://merchant.fizen.io/dashboard/payments)page, use the Status filter to view payments according to their status.&#x20;

Fizen Pay gives you the option to find your payments by customer email and invoice ID. You can use the search bar together with the filter to filter your payments in more detail.
