### Using credit on an order (30%)

Credit applies as a discount of **up to 30% of the eSIM plan price**, or your remaining balance if that is smaller.

- Plan **$10**, balance **$5** → **$3** credit applied, you pay **$7**, **$2** left
- Plan **$10**, balance **$2** → **$2** credit applied, you pay **$8**, **$0** left

After a successful purchase you receive **$2** more. That $2 is added after payment — it is not used on the order you just paid.

On some sign-up routes (including when you join through a partner), the **first** eSIM order is charged at the listed plan price and credit starts applying from the **next** eligible order. Your balance stays in the account for that next plan. Checkout always shows this before you pay.

## Promo codes

Promo codes are **unique to each site or campaign** and are **one-time use**. Admin issues a list of codes (for example one code per reader or per placement). Enter a valid unused code in the app and the code's value is added to your eSIM credit balance.

A claimed promo code cannot be reused on another device or account. Promo credit then follows the same 30% checkout rule as the rest of your balance.
