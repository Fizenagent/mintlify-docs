## The offer in one minute

Create your first Fizen account and you receive **$10 of eSIM credit** in the app. The credit is spent inside the Fizen App on eSIM data plans — you don't need a code, and you don't need to buy anything to receive it.

Three things worth knowing before you start:

- **It's credit, not cash.** It pays for eSIM plans in the app. It can't be withdrawn, transferred, or swapped for crypto.
- **It covers part of an order, not all of it.** Credit can cover **up to 30% of an order's value**; you pay the rest with USDT, card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.
- **It lasts a year.** Credit expires 12 months after it lands. Earning more credit pushes the expiry of your whole balance out to a year from that date.

    Promo codes are separate from welcome credit and each code can be used once. Whether both apply to the same order is shown at checkout.

    The offer is available wherever the Fizen App and Fizen eSIM are offered. It isn't available where the law restricts it, or in jurisdictions Fizen has excluded — see the Campaign Rules below.
