## How the credit is used (any eSIM credit)

Once credit is in your balance, the same spend rules apply, whether it came from the in-app welcome, a purchase reward, a referral, or a separate site/partner offer.

- On an eligible order, credit covers up to 30% of the plan price, or your remaining balance if that is smaller.
- Plan \$10, balance \$5 → \$3 applied, you pay \$7, \$2 left.
- Plan \$10, balance \$2 → \$2 applied, you pay \$8, \$0 left.
- After each successful purchase you get \$2 more. That amount is added after payment, so it is not used on the order you just paid.
- Credit lasts 12 months. Each new grant resets the expiry of your whole balance to 12 months from that day. Spending does not extend it.
- Credit is not cash. It cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or swapped for crypto or fiat.

<Info>
  Checkout always shows whether credit applies before you pay. On some partner sign-up routes, the first eSIM order is charged at the listed plan price and credit starts applying from the next eligible order.
</Info>
