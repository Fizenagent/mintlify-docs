## The promise

Every Fizen eSIM comes with a **1:1 Trip Guarantee**:

> If your eSIM has a problem on our side, we replace it 1-for-1 at **zero cost, for the entire length of your trip**, or refund you in full. Not just on day one: the guarantee covers you until you fly home.

And if your plans simply change, you can return an unused eSIM for a refund with a flat **$1** processing fee (where the plan allows refunds — see below).

---

## Something not working? Start here

Good news first: the vast majority of "my eSIM has no internet" cases are a small settings toggle, fixed in under two minutes. Before anything else, run the [2-minute checklist](/help-center/fizen-esim/troubleshooting): turn on Data Roaming, make sure the eSIM line is switched on and set as your data line.

If the issue continues, contact support and we'll investigate it under the 1:1 Trip Guarantee.

## How to request a replacement or refund

Everything happens in one chat. No forms, no email tickets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the live chat">
    Tap the **chat icon** in the Fizen App. A human agent will assist you as soon as possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your Order ID and eSIM details">
    So we can resolve it in one go, include:

    - **Order ID** (in your purchase confirmation)
    - **ICCID** of the eSIM (on the eSIM details screen in **My eSIMs**, under Manual installation, with a copy button)
    - **Device model** (e.g. iPhone 15, Galaxy S24)
    - A **screenshot** of the issue, if there is one
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get it fixed, replaced, or refunded">
    The agent first walks you through any quick setting fix. If the eSIM itself is at fault, you get a **free replacement on the spot** or a **full refund**. If a refund is approved, we'll confirm the available refund method based on how you paid.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When you get a free 1:1 replacement

| Situation | What you get |
|---|---|
| eSIM won't activate even though settings are correct | Free 1-for-1 replacement, or full refund |
| No service or signal at your destination | Free 1-for-1 replacement, or full refund |
| Data speed far below what the plan advertises | Replacement or partial refund, case by case |
| Your device turns out not to support eSIM | Full refund |
| eSIM stops working mid-trip | Free replacement, guarantee lasts your whole trip |

Replacements under the guarantee are always **free**. You never pay for a plan that didn't work.

## Returning an eSIM you no longer need

Bought the wrong country by mistake? Trip cancelled? Changed your mind close to departure? Unused eSIMs can usually be returned.

- **Fee:** a flat **$1** per eSIM, deducted from the refund
- **Refund method:** confirmed by our agent based on how you paid
- **Condition:** the eSIM hasn't been activated at the destination yet
- **Availability:** refund eligibility depends on the plan. Check whether your plan is **refundable before activation** in its **Package details**, or ask in the live chat before or right after buying and we'll confirm for your destination

<Note>
  **100% human support, zero guarantee fees.** Every conversation is with a real person, not a bot. Replacements under the 1:1 Trip Guarantee never cost you anything.
</Note>
