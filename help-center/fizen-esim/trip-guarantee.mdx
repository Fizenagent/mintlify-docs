## The promise

Every Fizen eSIM comes with a **1:1 Trip Guarantee**:

> If your eSIM doesn't connect at your destination — for any reason — we'll replace it **1-for-1 with zero fees**, or give you a **full refund**. No questions asked.

---

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrive at your destination">
    If your eSIM doesn't connect after following the activation steps, open the Fizen App.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Tap the **chat icon** in the bottom-right corner. You'll be connected to a human agent in under 3 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get replaced or refunded — free">
    Our agent will issue a replacement eSIM immediately at **no extra cost**, or process a full refund to your Fizen wallet balance. You never pay for a plan that didn't work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## What's covered

| Scenario | Resolution |
|---|---|
| eSIM doesn't activate at destination | Free 1-for-1 replacement or full refund |
| Wrong country plan purchased | Free replacement with correct plan |
| Data speed significantly below advertised | Case-by-case — replacement or partial refund |
| Device not compatible (discovered after purchase) | Full refund |

<Note>
  **100% human support, 0 fees** — every conversation is with a real person, and replacements under the guarantee never cost you anything. Average response time: under 3 minutes.
</Note>
