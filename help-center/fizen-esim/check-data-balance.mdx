title: "Check Your Data Balance"
description: "See how much data is left on your Fizen eSIM, and what to do before it runs out."
---

## Check in the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fizen App → eSIM">
    From the home screen, tap **eSIM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap My eSIMs">
    You'll see every eSIM you own with its status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your active plan">
    The detail screen shows **data remaining**, **data used**, and the plan's **validity period**, plus the eSIM's ICCID if support ever asks for it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Usage stats can take a few minutes to sync. Your phone's built-in data counter measures the whole line since it was last reset, so it usually won't match the plan balance exactly. The app is the accurate source.
</Info>

---

## Running low?

Buy a new plan in a few taps from the same **eSIM** screen. It activates on the eSIM you already installed, so there's nothing to set up again.

<Note>
  Buy the top-up **before** the old plan runs out and you'll stay connected the whole time, no gap, no hunting for Wi-Fi.
</Note>

---

## Make your data last longer

- Turn off **background app refresh** and **auto-updates** while traveling
- Watch **hotspot sharing**, it drains a plan faster than anything else
- Set video streaming apps to lower quality on mobile data
- Maps work offline: download your city's map on hotel Wi-Fi first
