## How to check

Go to **Settings → Cellular/Mobile Data → Add eSIM** on your phone. If this option appears, your device supports eSIM.

---

## Compatible devices

### Apple
- iPhone XS, XR and all newer models
- iPad Pro (3rd gen and later), iPad Air (3rd gen and later), iPad (7th gen and later)

### Samsung
- Galaxy S20 and newer
- Galaxy Z Flip / Z Fold series
- Galaxy Note 20 and newer
- Galaxy A54 and newer

### Google
- Pixel 3 and newer

### Other
- Huawei P40 and newer
- Xiaomi 12T Pro and newer
- OPPO Find X5 and newer
- Motorola Razr (2019) and newer

<Warning>
  **Carrier-locked phones** may not support eSIM even if the model is listed above. Check with your carrier if you're unsure.
</Warning>
