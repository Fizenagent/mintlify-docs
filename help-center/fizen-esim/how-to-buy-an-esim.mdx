---
title: "How to Buy an eSIM"
description: "Buy and activate a Fizen eSIM in under 2 minutes."
---

<Note>
  **Buy before you fly.** Activate your eSIM before departure so you're connected the moment you land.
</Note>

## Purchase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fizen App">
    Go to the home screen and tap **eSIM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search your destination">
    Type the country name. You'll see available data plans with pricing in USDT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick based on data amount, validity period, and coverage area. Regional plans cover multiple countries in one package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay with USDT">
    Select your payment token, confirm, and you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check notification">
    After purchase, you'll receive activation instructions via in-app notification and email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add eSIM to your phone">
    Go to **Settings → Cellular/Mobile → Add eSIM** on your device and follow the prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on data roaming">
    Enable **Data Roaming** for your new eSIM line in your phone's cellular settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Most eSIMs activate within 1–2 minutes. Some destinations may take up to 10 minutes.
</Info>
