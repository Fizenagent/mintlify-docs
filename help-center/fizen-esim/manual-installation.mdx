title: "Manual Installation"
description: "Install your Fizen eSIM by QR code or by entering the activation code manually — step-by-step for iOS and every Android brand."
---

<Note>
  Most users don't need this page — the [one-click activation link](/help-center/fizen-esim/how-to-buy-an-esim#activate-in-one-click) installs everything automatically. Use manual installation when you're installing the eSIM on a different phone than the one you bought it on (each eSIM still works on **one device only**), the link doesn't open, or you prefer QR/code entry.

  Your **QR code**, **SM-DP+ Address**, and **Activation Code** are all on the **eSIM detail screen** in the Fizen App, and in your confirmation email.
</Note>

    ### Enter details manually

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM → 'Use QR Code'">
        Same path as the QR flow (screens 1–3 above).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap 'Enter Details Manually' at the bottom of the screen">
        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/esim/ios-4.png" alt="Scan screen with Enter Details Manually link at the bottom" width="220" />
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the SM-DP+ Address and Activation Code">

        <Frame>

      </Step>

## After installing

Enable **Data Roaming** for the eSIM line and set it as your **mobile data** line when you arrive. Not connecting? Run the [2-minute troubleshooting checklist](/help-center/fizen-esim/troubleshooting) — and remember every plan is covered by the [1:1 Trip Guarantee](/help-center/fizen-esim/trip-guarantee).
