title: "Manual Installation"
description: "Install your Fizen eSIM by QR code or by entering the activation code manually — step-by-step for iOS and every Android brand."
---

<Note>
  Most users don't need this page — the [one-click activation link](/help-center/fizen-esim/how-to-buy-an-esim#activate-in-one-click) installs everything automatically. Use manual installation when you're setting up on a second device, the link doesn't open, or you prefer QR/code entry.

  Your **QR code**, **SM-DP+ Address**, and **Activation Code** are all on the **eSIM detail screen** in the Fizen App, and in your confirmation email.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    ### Via QR code

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings → Cellular (or Mobile Data)" />
      <Step title="Tap Add eSIM" />
      <Step title="Choose 'Use QR Code'" />
      <Step title="Scan the QR code from the eSIM detail screen">
        You'll need the QR on a second device or a printed copy.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Continue to activate the eSIM" />
      <Step title="Choose labels for your new eSIM line" />
      <Step title="Enable 'Turn On This Line' and 'Data Roaming'" />
    </Steps>

    ### Enter details manually

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings → Cellular (or Mobile Data)" />
      <Step title="Tap Add eSIM → 'Use QR Code'" />
      <Step title="Tap 'Enter Details Manually' at the bottom of the screen" />
      <Step title="Paste the SM-DP+ Address and Activation Code">
        Copy both from the eSIM detail screen in the Fizen App.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for activation to complete" />
      <Step title="Choose labels for your new eSIM line" />
      <Step title="Enable 'Turn On This Line' and 'Data Roaming'" />
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      If you can't connect after installing, configure the **Cellular Data Network APN** settings.
    </Info>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Samsung">
    ### Via QR code

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings → Connections" />
      <Step title="Tap SIM manager → Add eSIM" />

      <Step title="Tap Download (or Add)" />
      <Step title="Wait while the eSIM downloads, then tap Done" />
    </Steps>

    ### Enter code manually

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings → Connections → SIM manager" />
      <Step title="Tap Add eSIM → 'Enter activation code'" />
      <Step title="Enter the activation code and tap Next" />
      <Step title="Tap Download" />
      <Step title="Wait while the eSIM downloads, then tap Done" />
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

## After installing

Enable **Data Roaming** for the eSIM line and set it as your **mobile data** line when you arrive. Not connecting? Run the [2-minute troubleshooting checklist](/help-center/fizen-esim/troubleshooting) — and remember every plan is covered by the [1:1 Trip Guarantee](/help-center/fizen-esim/trip-guarantee).
