<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does my eSIM come with a phone number?">
    No — Fizen eSIMs are **data-only**. You keep your regular number active on your physical SIM for calls and SMS, and use the eSIM for internet. WhatsApp, Telegram, and iMessage keep working with your existing number.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I pay?">
    Your choice: **USDT** straight from your Fizen wallet balance, **Card (Visa/Mastercard)**, or **Apple Pay / Google Pay** for one-tap checkout. Card payments are processed by Stripe; Fizen never stores your card number. No bank account required for the USDT route.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When should I install and turn on my eSIM?">
    Install **shortly before departure** on a stable connection, ideally a trusted Wi-Fi network. Installing after arrival can fail because of provider restrictions or public Wi-Fi limitations.

    One important exception: if you bought a **regional or global plan that also covers the country you're in now**, don't turn it on early. Keep the eSIM line **off** and unselected for mobile data until you reach your destination — connecting to a supported network can start the plan's validity period and data usage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does my plan's validity start?">
    Validity starts when the eSIM **first connects to a supported network** — not at purchase. Buy days ahead and install before you fly, but keep the line off until you need it if your plan covers the country you're departing from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if my eSIM doesn't connect?">
    Check that you're in a covered destination, the eSIM line is **turned on** and **selected for mobile data**, and **Data Roaming** is on for the eSIM. Then toggle **Airplane Mode for 30 seconds** or restart your device. Still nothing? Check the **APN and network instructions** in your plan, follow the full [troubleshooting checklist](/help-center/fizen-esim/troubleshooting), or contact Fizen Support in the app.

    **Whatever you do, don't delete the eSIM** — a deleted eSIM can't be reinstalled, but a "not working" one can almost always be fixed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use one eSIM on multiple devices?">
    No. Each eSIM works on **one device only**. Install it on the device you'll actually use for your trip — once activated, it can't be transferred to another device.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I share my connection via hotspot?">
    Yes — hotspot/tethering is supported. Keep in mind that sharing drains your data allowance faster.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when my data runs out?">
    Buy a new plan in the Fizen App in a few taps — it activates on the same eSIM. Check your remaining balance anytime in **eSIM → My eSIMs** ([guide](/help-center/fizen-esim/check-data-balance)).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use one plan in multiple countries?">
    Yes, with a **regional plan** — one package covers every country in the region (e.g. Europe, Asia). Single-country plans work only in that country.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I return an eSIM I haven't used?">
    Yes. If you bought the wrong country, your trip got cancelled, or you simply changed your mind, unused eSIMs can be returned for a refund to your Fizen wallet with a flat **$1** processing fee. Over 90% of destinations are refundable; a few (such as Thailand) don't allow refunds. Just message the **in-app live chat** with your Order ID. Details: [1:1 Trip Guarantee & Refunds](/help-center/fizen-esim/trip-guarantee).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I deleted my eSIM by accident — can I get it back?">
    A deleted eSIM can't be reinstalled from your side. Contact support via the **in-app live chat** — under the [1:1 Trip Guarantee](/help-center/fizen-esim/trip-guarantee), we'll issue a replacement for any unused data at no cost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my regular SIM and eSIM at the same time?">
    Yes, if your device supports **Dual SIM** and is carrier-unlocked. Use the Fizen eSIM for mobile data and keep your regular SIM active for calls and texts. To avoid surprise roaming charges: turn **off Data Roaming for your regular SIM** and disable **Cellular Data Switching** (iOS: Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data). Note your home carrier may still charge for calls or texts you make abroad.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much data do I need?">
    Rough guide: maps + messaging ≈ 0.5 GB/day, social media + photos ≈ 1 GB/day, video calls + streaming ≈ 2+ GB/day. When in doubt, take the bigger plan — Fizen's per-GB pricing is the cheapest on the market.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
