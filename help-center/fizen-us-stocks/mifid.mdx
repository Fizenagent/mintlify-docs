title: "MiFID II (European Union)"
description: "How the EU Markets in Financial Instruments Directive applies to tokenized securities offered in the EU."
---

**MiFID** (the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive, now MiFID II, Directive 2014/65/EU) is the European Union framework that regulates financial services, protects investors and promotes transparency in securities trading.

For tokenized assets offered in the EU market:

- they must be distributed through registered platforms that comply with MiFID II;
- information on the product, its costs, its risks and investor rights must be transparent;
- there must be mechanisms for dispute resolution and for supervising distribution.

## What this means for you

<Info>
  Where a token counts as a "financial instrument" under MiFID II, it falls outside the MiCA crypto-asset regime and is governed by MiFID II and national law. **Fizen does not provide investment services under MiFID II and is not authorised as an investment firm in any EEA member state.** See section 12 of the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa).
</Info>
