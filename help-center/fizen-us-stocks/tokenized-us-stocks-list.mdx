Tokenized stocks are Ethereum tokens issued by Ondo that track the price of a share listed in the U.S. market, including U.S.-listed shares of companies based outside the U.S. Each token name is the share ticker followed by "on" (for example, AAPLon tracks Apple, ticker AAPL, and ANTon tracks Anthropic, ticker ANT).

<Warning>
  Tokenized stocks and ETFs are an investment, not a savings product. Prices can fall as well as rise, and you can lose some or all of what you put in. This list is information only and is not a recommendation or financial advice. Not available to U.S. Persons. See the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa).
</Warning>
