<Warning>
  Investing, not saving: the value of what you buy can fall, and you can lose some or all of it. Not available to U.S. Persons. Nothing here is financial advice. By trading you accept the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa).
</Warning>

## Before you start

- You have a [Fizen wallet](/help-center/fizen-us-stocks/create-a-fizen-wallet).
- You hold **USDT or USDC on Ethereum** in that wallet, plus a little **ETH** for network (gas) fees. The app shows which stablecoins you can pay with.
- Trades run on the **Ethereum** network. Select Ethereum as the network in the app.

## Buy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open U.S. Stocks">
    Open the app and tap **U.S. Stock** on the home screen.
    <Frame caption="U.S. Stock on the Fizen Super App home screen">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/us-stocks-feature.jpg" alt="Fizen Super App home screen with the U.S. Stock feature highlighted" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to pay with, what to buy, and how much">
    On the **Buy** tab, pick the stablecoin you pay with (**Pay**), the tokenized stock you want (**Receive**), and enter the amount. Check the rate, the slippage tolerance, the **minimum receive** amount and the transaction fee, then tap **Next**.
    <Frame caption="Enter the amount to buy the selected tokenized U.S. stock">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/buy-2.png" alt="Buy screen paying USDC to receive TSLAon, showing rate, slippage, minimum receive and transaction fee" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the order on the **Review order** screen: network, amount you pay, amount you receive, slippage tolerance, minimum receive, transaction fee and estimated gas fee. The quote refreshes on a timer. Tap **Confirm** to sign the transaction in your wallet.
    <Frame caption="Confirm the purchase">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/buy-3.png" alt="Review order screen on Ethereum with a Confirm button" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order created">
    The app creates your buy order. You can follow it on the **Order information** screen.
    <Frame caption="Buy order created">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/buy-4.png" alt="Order information screen showing Creating your order" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order matched and delivered">
    Your order is matched on the market, usually within a few minutes (the app shows an estimate, for example 3 to 10 minutes). The tokenized stock is then delivered to your Fizen wallet.
    <Frame caption="Order being matched">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/buy-5.png" alt="Order information screen showing Processing your order with a filled amount bar" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Partly filled orders.** If only part of your order is matched, you are charged only for the tokens actually bought, and you receive those tokens. See the [FAQ](/help-center/fizen-us-stocks/faq).
</Info>

<Note>
  The price you receive is set on-chain by 1inch Fusion and the liquidity providers, not by Fizen. It can differ from the quote within your slippage tolerance, and liquidity can be thinner outside U.S. market hours.
</Note>
