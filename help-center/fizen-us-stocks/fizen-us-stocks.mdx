The **U.S. Stocks** feature in the Fizen Super App gives you access to **100+ tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs** issued by Ondo Finance. You buy and sell them with **USDT or USDC on Ethereum**, straight from your own Fizen wallet, with no brokerage account.

<Warning>
  **Investing, not saving.** Prices can fall as well as rise, and you can lose some or all of what you put in. Not available to U.S. Persons. Nothing in these guides is financial advice. Read the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa) before you trade.
</Warning>
