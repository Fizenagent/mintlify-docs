## Financial reports

For the latest financial reports of the underlying U.S. companies, see the [U.S. stocks financial reports sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1i2dhSpoxawKmcVQ6e9Z7R5RG1n1BoJiu/edit?gid=557723806#gid=557723806). You can also follow each underlying security through the Yahoo Finance link in the tables.

<Note>
  The catalogue in the app is read from Ondo and can change at any time. Fizen does not select, rank or recommend tokens. The app is the source of truth for what you can trade today, and you should always check the contract address in the app before you trade or send a token.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Being on this list is not a recommendation. Tokenized stocks and ETFs are an investment, not a savings product, and their value can fall. Not available to U.S. Persons. This is not financial advice.
</Warning>
