---
title: "Sell Tokenized U.S. Stocks"
description: "Step-by-step guide to selling tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs for a stablecoin in the Fizen Super App."
---

You can sell your tokenized U.S. stocks directly in the Fizen Super App and receive a stablecoin back in your wallet. Trades run on the **Ethereum** network, so keep a little **ETH** for network (gas) fees.

## Sell

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open U.S. Stocks">
    Open the app and tap **U.S. Stock** on the home screen. Make sure **Ethereum** is the selected network.
    <Frame caption="U.S. Stock on the Fizen Super App home screen">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/us-stocks-feature.jpg" alt="Fizen Super App home screen with the U.S. Stock feature highlighted" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sell and what to receive">
    Switch to the **Sell** tab. Pick the tokenized stock you want to sell (**Pay**), enter the number of tokens, and choose the stablecoin you want to receive (**Receive**). Tap **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    On the **Review order** screen, check the network, the amount you sell, the amount you receive, the rate, slippage tolerance, minimum receive and transaction fee. Tap **Confirm** to sign the transaction in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order created">
    The app creates your sell order. You can follow it on the **Order information** screen: **Order created**, **Processing**, **Delivered**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proceeds delivered">
    Once the order is matched, the stablecoin proceeds are sent to your Fizen wallet and the order shows as completed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Partly filled orders.** If only part of your sell order is matched, only the matched tokens leave your wallet, and you receive the value of the tokens actually sold. See the [FAQ](/help-center/fizen-us-stocks/faq).
</Info>

<Warning>
  The price you receive is set on-chain and can differ from the quote within your slippage tolerance. You may get back less than you paid. Not available to U.S. Persons. This guide is information only and is not financial advice. See the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa).
</Warning>
