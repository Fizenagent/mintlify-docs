---
title: "Create a Fizen Wallet"
description: "Download the Fizen Super App and set up your self-custody wallet before you trade tokenized U.S. stocks."
---

You need a Fizen wallet in the Fizen Super App to buy and sell tokenized U.S. stocks.

<Card title="Download the Fizen Super App" icon="download" href="https://fizen.io/app?accessCode=fizen&utm_source=web&utm_medium=fizen&utm_campaign=app_download&utm_content=docs_us_stocks&aid=6adeccbd730938bc9eb41e13" color="#6E3BFF">
  Available on the App Store and Google Play
</Card>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Go to the App Store or Google Play, search for **Fizen Super App** and install it.
    <Frame caption="Fizen Super App on the App Store">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/create-account-1.jpg" alt="Fizen Super App listing on the App Store" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your language">
    Open the app and set it to your preferred language.
    <Frame caption="Setting the app language">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/create-account-2.jpg" alt="Language selection screen in the Fizen Super App" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Set up Wallet">
    Tap **Set up Wallet** to start creating your personal Fizen wallet.
    <Frame caption="Fizen wallet setup">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/create-account-3.jpg" alt="Set up Wallet button on the welcome screen" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or import a wallet">
    Select **Create a new wallet** to generate a new Fizen wallet, or **Import existing wallet** to keep using one you already have.
    <Frame caption="Create a new wallet or import an existing one">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/create-account-4.jpg" alt="Options to create a new wallet or import an existing wallet" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password">
    Enter a password, confirm it, add a referral code if you have one, and tap **Create Wallet**.
    <Frame caption="Password and referral code">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/create-account-5.jpg" alt="Password, confirm password and referral code fields" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back up your wallet">
    Back up your wallet with a **passkey** (linked to your Apple or Google account) or **manually with your secret phrase**. The backup lets you recover your wallet if you change phone or reinstall the app.
    <Frame caption="Back up with a passkey or manually with your secret phrase">
      <img src="/images/us-stocks/create-account-6.jpg" alt="Backup with Passkey screen with an option to back up the secret phrase manually" width="260" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Backing up is mandatory.** If you back up manually, keep your 12-word secret phrase safe and never share it with anyone. The Fizen wallet is self-custody: Fizen does not store your secret phrase and cannot recover your wallet if you lose it.
</Warning>
