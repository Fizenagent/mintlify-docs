These tokens track U.S. bond ETFs. They follow the market price of the fund, which moves with interest rates and credit conditions, so their value can go down. They are not the same as holding a U.S. government bond directly, and bond funds still carry the risk of loss.

<Warning>
  Tokenized stocks and ETFs are an investment, not a savings product. Prices can fall as well as rise, and you can lose some or all of what you put in. This list is information only and is not a recommendation or financial advice. Not available to U.S. Persons. See the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa).
</Warning>
