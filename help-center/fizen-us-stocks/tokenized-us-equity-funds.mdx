These tokens track U.S.-listed exchange-traded funds (ETFs) that hold a basket of shares, such as funds that follow the S&P 500, the Russell indices or the Nasdaq-100, and sector or thematic funds. A few are leveraged or inverse funds (for example TQQQon, SQQQon, SOXLon and SOXSon), which aim for a multiple or the opposite of a daily index move. They can lose value quickly and are not meant to be held for long periods.

<Warning>
  Tokenized stocks and ETFs are an investment, not a savings product. Prices can fall as well as rise, and you can lose some or all of what you put in. This list is information only and is not a recommendation or financial advice. Not available to U.S. Persons. See the [Tokenized Securities Terms](/tokenized-rwa).
</Warning>
