Fizen Swap uses leading DEX aggregators -[ KyberSwap](https://kyberswap.com/) &[ 1inch](http://1inch.io).&#x20;

Swap is currently only available for Android devices.


**Step 1**: Tap the Swap button on the Homepage.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/13.Swap (Đổi token) 1.jpg>)

**Step 2**: Choose account & network

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/13.Swap (Đổi token) 2.jpg>)

**Step 3**: Input swap amount&#x20;

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/13.Swap (Đổi token) 3.jpg>)

**Step 4**: Review all your swap details before confirming all the transaction information.

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/13.Swap (Đổi token) 4.jpg>)
