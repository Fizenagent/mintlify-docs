---
title: "Swap"
---

Swap tokens directly in the Fizen app using leading DEX aggregators — **KyberSwap** and **1inch**.

Currently available on **Android** only.

---

## How to swap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Swap on the homepage">
    Find the Swap button on the main screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose account & network">
    Select the wallet account and blockchain network for the swap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter swap amount">
    Input the amount you want to swap and select the output token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check all details — token amounts, rates, fees — then confirm the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Swap rates are aggregated across multiple DEXs to give you the best available price.
</Note>
