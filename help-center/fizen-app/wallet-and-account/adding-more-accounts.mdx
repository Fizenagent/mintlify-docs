---
title: "Adding More Accounts"
---

Create multiple accounts within the same wallet to separate funds or manage different purposes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Wallet">Open the Fizen app and tap **Wallet**.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Account">Select **Add Account** or the **+** icon.</Step>
  <Step title="Name your account">Give the account a name to identify it easily.</Step>
  <Step title="Done">Your new account is ready with a unique wallet address. Switch between accounts from the selector at the top.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All accounts in one Fizen wallet share the same seed phrase. One backup covers all accounts.
</Note>
