---
title: "Import Token & NFT"
---

## Import a custom token

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Wallet">Open the Fizen app → **Wallet**.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Token">Scroll to bottom → tap **Add Token** or **+**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter contract address">Paste the token contract address. Name and symbol auto-fill.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Tap **Add**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Import an NFT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to NFTs tab">Navigate to the **NFTs** tab in your wallet.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Import NFT">Enter the NFT contract address and token ID.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">The NFT appears in your collection.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Make sure you're on the correct network before importing.
</Note>
