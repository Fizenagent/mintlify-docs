---
title: "Reset Wallet & Export Private Key"
---

## Export your private key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Security">Open the Fizen app → **Settings** → **Security**.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Export Private Key">Enter your PIN to authenticate.</Step>
  <Step title="Store securely">Copy your private key and store it offline. Never share it.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Reset your wallet

<Warning>
  Resetting removes all data from the app. Make sure you have your **seed phrase or private key** before proceeding.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Security">Find the Reset Wallet option.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm reset">Proceed only if you have your backup.</Step>
  <Step title="Restore">Re-import your 12-word seed phrase to restore access.</Step>
</Steps>
