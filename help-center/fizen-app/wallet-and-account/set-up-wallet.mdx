---
title: "Set Up Wallet"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Fizen App">
    Get the app on [iOS or Android](https://url.fizen.io/downloadgb?utm_source=docs_mintlify&utm_medium=docs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new wallet">
    Open the app and tap **Create New Wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back up your seed phrase">
    Write down your 12-word seed phrase and store it safely offline. **Never share it with anyone.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm seed phrase">
    Re-enter your seed phrase to confirm it's saved correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a PIN">
    Create a PIN to protect access on your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your seed phrase is the only way to recover your wallet. If lost, funds cannot be recovered by anyone — including Fizen.
</Warning>
