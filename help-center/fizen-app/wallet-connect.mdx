1. ### Scan QR

**Step 1**: From the Dapp on the other device, select Connect wallet and then the WalletConnect option.&#x20;

![](../../.gitbook/assets/WalletConnect.png)

**Step 2**: Tap **Scan**. This button is accessible from the homepage, wallet page, token page, or connected sites page.


**Step 3**: Grant permission to access the camera.

![](../../.gitbook/assets/Homepage.png)

**Step 4**: Scan QR code in Fizen

**Step 5**: Follow the prompts to finish connecting with Dapp.


2. ### Fizen in-app browser&#x20;

**Step 1**: Choose **Application > Browser** on the homepage and search for the Dapp you want to access.&#x20;

 

 

**Step 2**: Select Connect wallet and then select WalletConnect.&#x20;

**Step 3**: Tap **Select Wallet**.

![](../../.gitbook/assets/1698221477896.jpg)

**Step 4**: Follow the prompts to finish connecting with Dapp.


3. ### Common request from Dapps&#x20;

If you want to request "Sign message", "Approve token" and "Confirm transaction", please do not close the app in the middle of the WalletConnect sessions.
