---
title: "Wallet Connect"
---

Connect Fizen to external dApps using WalletConnect — scan a QR code or use the in-app browser.

---

## Method 1 — Scan QR Code

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the WalletConnect QR from the dApp">On the external device/browser, select **Connect Wallet → WalletConnect** to display the QR code.</Step>
  <Step title="Open Scan in Fizen">Tap **Scan** in the Fizen app — accessible from homepage, wallet page, or token page.</Step>
  <Step title="Grant camera permission">Allow camera access when prompted.</Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">Point your camera at the dApp's QR code.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve connection">Follow the prompts to confirm the connection.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Method 2 — Fizen In-App Browser

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Browser in Fizen">Tap **Application → Browser** on the homepage and search for the dApp.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect wallet">Select **Connect Wallet → WalletConnect → Select Wallet**.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve connection">Follow prompts to finish connecting.</Step>
</Steps>
