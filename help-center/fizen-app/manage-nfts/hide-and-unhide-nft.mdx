---
title: "Hide & Unhide NFT"
---

## Hide an NFT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to NFTs tab">Fizen App → Wallet → **NFTs**.</Step>
  <Step title="Long press the NFT">Hold down on the NFT you want to hide.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Hide">Tap **Hide** from the options menu.</Step>
</Steps>

## Unhide an NFT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to NFTs tab">Tap the filter or settings icon to show hidden NFTs.</Step>
  <Step title="Find the hidden NFT">Locate the NFT you want to restore.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Unhide">The NFT reappears in your main collection.</Step>
</Steps>
