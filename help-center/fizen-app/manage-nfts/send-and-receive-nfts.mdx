---
title: "Send & Receive NFTs"
---

## Send an NFT

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to NFTs tab">Fizen App → Wallet → **NFTs**.</Step>
  <Step title="Select the NFT">Tap on the NFT you want to send.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Send">Enter the recipient's wallet address or scan their QR code.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Review and confirm. Gas fees apply.</Step>
</Steps>

## Receive an NFT

Share your wallet address with the sender. Your NFT appears in the **NFTs** tab once the transaction confirms on-chain.

<Warning>
  Always verify the sender before accepting NFT transfers. Malicious NFTs can be used in phishing attacks.
</Warning>
