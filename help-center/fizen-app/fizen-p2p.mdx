**Selling cryptocurrencies **

**Step 1**: Tap the **Sell** section to sell your cryptocurrencies for fiat currency, and enter the amount you want to sell.

 

Fizen P2P supports buying and selling various types of cryptocurrencies. You can choose the cryptocurrency type before purchasing by tapping on the cryptocurrency icon. After entering the amount you want to buy, press **Next**.

**Step 2**: Enter the bank account information where you want to receive the fiat currency. Press **Next**.


**Step 3**: Carefully review the information displayed on your phone screen. Then, slide the horizontal bar at the bottom of the screen to confirm.&#x20;

The funds will be transferred to the bank account you selected after a few minutes.

 

**NOTE**: The funds may be transferred to your account later than expected, and Fizen will notify you when the transaction is successful.
