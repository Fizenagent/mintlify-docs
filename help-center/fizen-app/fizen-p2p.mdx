---
title: "Fizen P2P"
---


One of the useful features of the Fizen Super App is Fizen P2P, where you can buy/sell cryptocurrencies using fiat currency.

First, you need to go to the **Fizen P2P** section on the main screen.


Alternatively, you can go to the **Buy & Sell** section and then click on **Fizen P2P**.


{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Buying cryptocurrencies" %}
**Step 1:** Touch the **Buy** section and enter the fiat amount you want to spend to buy cryptocurrencies.&#x20;

 

The conversion rate will be updated by Fizen from the provider every 30 seconds. Fizen always updates the best exchange rate for you so that you can buy the most with the least cost.

Fizen P2P supports the purchase and sale of various cryptocurrencies. You can select the cryptocurrency type before purchasing by tapping on the cryptocurrency icon.

After entering the amount you want to buy, press the **Next** button to proceed to the next screen.

**Step 2:** Enter the address where you want to receive the cryptocurrency.

Fizen will automatically fill in the wallet address as the address of the account you are logged into.&#x20;

You can choose other accounts in your wallet or scan a QR code.&#x20;

Then, press **Next**.

 

**Step 3**: Confirm the transaction.

After carefully reviewing the information before payment, swipe the horizontal bar at the bottom of the screen to confirm.


**Step 4:** Make the payment.

To make the payment, you need to transfer money from your bank account to the account displayed on the screen.

 

**NOTE**: **You need to make sure that the message content when transferring funds exactly matches the note section**. Even a small mistake can lead to the payment not being executed.&#x20;

Once you have completed the transfer, press **I have transferred the funds**.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Selling cryptocurrencies " %}
**Step 1**: Tap the **Sell** section to sell your cryptocurrencies for fiat currency, and enter the amount you want to sell.

 

Fizen P2P supports buying and selling various types of cryptocurrencies. You can choose the cryptocurrency type before purchasing by tapping on the cryptocurrency icon. After entering the amount you want to buy, press **Next**.

**Step 2**: Enter the bank account information where you want to receive the fiat currency. Press **Next**.


**Step 3**: Carefully review the information displayed on your phone screen. Then, slide the horizontal bar at the bottom of the screen to confirm.&#x20;

The funds will be transferred to the bank account you selected after a few minutes.

 

**NOTE**: The funds may be transferred to your account later than expected, and Fizen will notify you when the transaction is successful.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
