---
title: "Fizen P2P"
---

Buy and sell cryptocurrency using local fiat currency — directly within the Fizen app.

---

## How to buy crypto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Fizen P2P">
    Tap **Fizen P2P** on the homepage, or go to **Buy & Sell → Fizen P2P**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount">
    Tap **Buy**, enter the fiat amount you want to spend. Select the cryptocurrency you want to receive. Exchange rates update every 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter recipient address">
    Fizen auto-fills your wallet address. You can change it or scan a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm transaction">
    Review the details and swipe to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## How to sell crypto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Fizen P2P">
    Tap **Sell** in the Fizen P2P section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount">
    Enter the crypto amount you want to sell and select your payout method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm transaction">
    Review and confirm. Fiat will be credited to your selected account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Fizen always provides the best available exchange rate, updated in real time from providers.
</Note>
