1. WHAT IS FIPOINT?

**FiPoint** is Fizen’s loyalty reward points that can be redeemed for real-life gifts. You can get vouchers for café, restaurants, movie theaters, and mobile top-up.

Fipoint is not a cryptocurrency, but instead an onramp for potential Web3 users. If you are interested in the crypto space but hesitate to spend, FiPoint is the perfect training ground for new users.&#x20;

To eliminate the financial barrier and encourage Web3 usage, Fizen is free to earn through in-app activities and cannot be bought.


2. UTILITIES OF FIPOINT

FiPoint is not just a loyalty point system, it’s an incentive to attract Web2 users to onboard Web3 via Fizen. Some of the current and future utilities of FiPoint are:

💎Redeeming gift cards from popular brands

💎Redeeming for exclusive NFT&#x20;

💎Staking up to 30% APY

💎Playing Spin & Win games
