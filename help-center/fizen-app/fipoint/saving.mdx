title: "Saving"
---


With the Fizen saving plan, we offer FiPoint loyalty point savings with compound interest of up to 30%, without any blockchain transaction fees.

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Deposit " %}
**Step 1**: Tap **Saving** on **Homepage**

![](../../../.gitbook/assets/homepage.png)

**Step 2**: Choose the saving source and token to start

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/photo_2023-12-14_14-20-08 (2).jpg>)

**Step 3**: Enter the saving amount to deposit. The interest will fluctuate based on the market condition (\~30%)

 

**Step 4**: Select **Confirm**

**Step 5:** Wait for transaction to process. You will be notified once the deposit is completed.


{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Withdraw " %}
**Step 1**: Tap **Saving** on **Homepage**

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/homepage (1).png>)

**Step 2**: You will be prompted to My Savings screen once you have deposited the asset. Select the asset you want to withdraw


**Step 3**: Select **Withdraw** button

![](../../../.gitbook/assets/withdraw.png)

**Step 4**: You will be prompted to the Withdraw screen. Input withdraw amount.&#x20;


**Step 5**: Select **Confirm**

**Step 6**: Wait for transaction to process. You will be notified once the withdrawal is completed.


{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
