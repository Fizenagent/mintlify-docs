**Withdraw **

**Step 1**: Tap **Saving** on **Homepage**


**Step 2**: You will be prompted to My Savings screen once you have deposited the asset. Select the asset you want to withdraw


**Step 3**: Select **Withdraw** button

![](../../../.gitbook/assets/withdraw.png)

**Step 4**: You will be prompted to the Withdraw screen. Input withdraw amount.&#x20;


**Step 5**: Select **Confirm**

**Step 6**: Wait for transaction to process. You will be notified once the withdrawal is completed.
