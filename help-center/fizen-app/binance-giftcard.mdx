---
title: "Binance Gift Cards"
---

Buy gift cards with crypto and send them to anyone — all within the Fizen app.

---

## Buy a gift card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse gift cards">
    Go to the Gift Cards section and choose from available brands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to cart">
    Select the cards you want and add them to your cart. You can buy multiple at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose payment token">
    Select which crypto you want to pay with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Confirm and pay. Your gift cards are ready instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

[Watch: How to buy multiple gift cards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5iUZGkyR5oA)

---

## Send a gift card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Giftcard">
    Select **My Giftcard** on the homepage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select cards to send">
    Long-press on a card or tap **Send gifts** to select multiple cards at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipient">
    Select from contacts, recent activity, or enter/scan a wallet address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the send. The recipient gets notified when the gift is delivered.
  </Step>
</Steps>
