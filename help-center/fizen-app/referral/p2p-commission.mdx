---
title: "P2P Commission"
---

Earn commission automatically when friends you've referred complete P2P transactions.

| Level | Who | Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Your direct referrals | % of their P2P transaction fee |
| Level 2 | People referred by your referrals | Smaller % of their P2P transaction fee |

Commission is credited to your Fizen wallet automatically after each qualifying trade.

Check the **Referral** section in the app for your exact rates and earnings history.
