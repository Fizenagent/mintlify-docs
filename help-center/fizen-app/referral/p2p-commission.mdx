title: "P2P Commission"
---


Invite your friends to use Fizen P2P and earn:

* USDT commission
* FiPoint cashback benefits

1. **USDT commission**

Invite your friends to use Fizen P2P and you will earn **1% USDT commission** for each transaction from your invitees. Rewards will be distributed on the fifth day of each month.


2. **FiPoint Cashback Benefits from Buy/Sell P2P**

 

* If you got a P2P invitation from our partners or users, a small part of your purchase will be given to your referrer, and you will also receive FiPoint.

&#x20;      _Maximum 50,000 FiPoint per transaction_

* If you make P2P transaction by yourself, you will receive 1% FiPoint cashback from your purchase.

&#x20;      _Maximum 50,000 FiPoint per transaction_
