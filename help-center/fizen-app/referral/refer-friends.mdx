---
title: "Refer Friends"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invite Friends">On the homepage, scroll down and select **Invite Friends to Earn 6,000 FiPoints**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to share">
    - **Referral Code** — copy and share (you can customize it by tapping **Edit**)
    - **Referral Link** — copy and share directly
    - **QR Code** — save or share the QR image
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend signs up">Your friend downloads Fizen and registers using your code, link, or QR.</Step>
  <Step title="Both of you earn">You receive FiPoints and commission. Your friend gets their welcome bonus.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Check the **Referral** section in the app for your current commission rate and referral stats.
</Note>
