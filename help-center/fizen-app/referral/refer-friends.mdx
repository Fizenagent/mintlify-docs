---
title: "Refer Friends"
description: "Share your Fizen referral code. You and your friend each get $10 eSIM credit — you can earn this on up to 10 friends — plus FiPoints and commission."
---

When a friend enters your referral code, **both of you receive $10 eSIM credit**. You can earn the inviter $10 on **up to 10** friends. After that, new friends still get their $10.

Referral credit sits in the same eSIM balance as [welcome credit](/help-center/fizen-esim/welcome-credit). On eligible orders it can cover **up to 30%** of the plan price.

<Card title="Start on fizen.io/esim — $10 website credit" icon="gift" href="https://fizen.io/esim?utm_source=docs_mintlify&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=esim10" color="#6E3BFF">
  Ask your friend to claim on the website first, then open the app with your code. New users who do both can stack website $10 + in-app $10 + referral $10.
</Card>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invite Friends">On the homepage, scroll down and select **Invite Friends**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to share">
    - **Referral Code** — copy and share (you can customize it by tapping **Edit**)
    - **Referral Link** — copy and share directly
    - **QR Code** — save or share the QR image
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend claims on the website, then signs up">
    Send them [fizen.io/esim](https://fizen.io/esim?utm_source=docs_mintlify&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=esim10) so they can claim the website $10, then have them download Fizen and enter your code, link, or QR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Both of you earn eSIM credit">
    They receive **$10** eSIM credit. You receive **$10** eSIM credit (up to 10 times). They also get the automatic in-app welcome if they are a new user. You both keep earning FiPoints and commission as before.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Check the **Referral** section in the app for your current commission rate, FiPoint rewards, and how many of your 10 eSIM referral rewards you have used.
</Note>
