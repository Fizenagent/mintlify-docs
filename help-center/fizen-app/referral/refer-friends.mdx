---
title: "Refer Friends"
description: "Share your Fizen referral code. You and your friend each get USD 10 eSIM credit, up to 10 friends, plus FiPoints and commission."
---

When a friend enters your **personal** referral code, **both of you receive USD 10 eSIM credit**. You can earn the inviter USD 10 on **up to 10** friends. After that, new friends still get their USD 10.

This is **separate** from the [in-app welcome credit](/help-center/fizen-esim/welcome-credit). A new friend who downloads the app from 19 August 2026 can still get the automatic USD 10 welcome **and** the referral USD 10.

<Card title="Send them the app" icon="gift" href="https://fizen.io/app?accessCode=VJBw&utm_source=web&utm_medium=fizen&utm_campaign=app_download&utm_content=docs_referral&aid=6a6c68c37102b97a3142b253" color="#6E3BFF">
  New users get USD 10 eSIM credit automatically in the app. Your personal code adds USD 10 more for each of you.
</Card>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invite Friends">On the homepage, scroll down and select **Invite Friends**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to share">
    - **Referral Code** — copy and share (you can customize it by tapping **Edit**)
    - **Referral Link** — copy and share directly
    - **QR Code** — save or share the QR image
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend downloads the app">
    They install Fizen, create their first account, and enter your code, link, or QR. They do **not** need to visit fizen.io/esim for the in-app welcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Both of you earn eSIM credit">
    They receive **USD 10**. You receive **USD 10** (up to 10 times). If they are a new user from 19 August 2026, the automatic in-app welcome is added as well. FiPoints and commission continue as before.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Check **Referral** in the app for commission, FiPoints, and how many of your 10 eSIM inviter grants you have used. The cap counts referrals in the current campaign period, and only **personal codes**. Installs through promotional or company-issued links do not generate referral credit (your friend can still get the automatic in-app welcome).
</Note>
