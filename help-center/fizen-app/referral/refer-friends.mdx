---
title: "Refer friends"
---


Intending to expand the active user community, Fizen has included the referral code feature in the app, thereby giving the newbie some attractive welcome gifts and, at the same time, offering many exclusive rewards for the referrers.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (143).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. **How to refer your friends?**

Get up to 5,000 FiPoint for each successful referral, by following these simple steps:

* Share your Referral Link or Referral Code with friends.
* After your friends finish installing Fizen, open the app and then import or create a new wallet. Make sure your friend input the correct Referral Code when creating account. Another way is having your friends scan your QR code to confirm the referral.
* After your friends' first transaction on Fizen (buying Gift Cards, using P2P), both of you will receive 10,000 FiPoint.

2. **What a successful referral?**

* Your friends have to import an existing wallet or create a new one on Fizen
* Your friend must make at least 1 transaction on Fizen (buying Gift Cards, using P2P)
