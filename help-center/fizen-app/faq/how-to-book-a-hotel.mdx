# How to book a hotel?

Follow these steps to book a hotel room:

**Step 1:** On the **Homepage** of the **Fizen Super App**, select the **Travel** section.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2F9c0oILQUZJWY1ry0bkRa%2Fz6655922262952_764cace29badfb2f0ad3240eb124df54.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=8b551d80-6078-418f-b2ee-63b371cd8447" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 2:** Tap the **Search** bar and enter the **destination** or **hotel name** you're looking for.

**Step 3:** Fill in the following details:

* **Number of rooms**, and number of **guests** (adults and children)
* **Length of stay**
* **Nationality**

Then tap **Search**

<div align="left"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2FECGtrfrgvLciWfduF01V%2Fz6655922230481_235f49bfe279093ccc43ac42051ff153.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=7fddc8a9-aabc-49a2-a822-6321cce353b3" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 4:** Browse the hotel list and select the one you like to view detailed information.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2FlanBVNCSVTZVlzdsnYPa%2Fz6655922242673_058e3b2b7c1eac1c6b21d43d6aa640c0.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=4e496920-9723-49b6-aa09-2e09afbf68bd" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 5:** Choose the **room type** you want to proceed with the booking.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2FqfGkmt0W7ZJimlzSZz5f%2Fz6655922232952_e024b7fcee64d2709352057aa593f9ec.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=401bf647-89ad-4529-a23e-929391579ba4" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 6:** Fill in your personal details:

* **Full name**
* **Email address**
* **Phone number**
* **Nationality**

<div align="left"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2FsNeAC1BjEkThIS0j0tpC%2Fz6655922222096_05bb0de146a2645dec9ce6358a03522b.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=d0f35514-55a0-4f86-83d7-8c39bf49709f" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Step 7:** Select your preferred **payment method**: BNB or USDT

Then tap **Pay** and **Swipe to confirm**
