---
title: "How to Book a Hotel"
---

Book hotels across multiple countries directly from the Fizen Super App.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Travel section">On the homepage, select **Travel**.</Step>
  <Step title="Search destination">Tap the **Search** bar and enter your destination or hotel name.</Step>
  <Step title="Fill in stay details">Enter number of rooms, guests (adults and children), length of stay, and nationality. Tap **Search**.</Step>
  <Step title="Browse and select">View the hotel list and tap a hotel to see detailed information.</Step>
  <Step title="Select room and complete booking">Choose your room type, review the details, and complete payment with crypto.</Step>
</Steps>
