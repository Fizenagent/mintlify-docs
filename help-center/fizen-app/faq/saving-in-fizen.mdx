---
title: "Saving in Fizen"
---

Fizen offers two savings options:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fizen Savings** | FiPoint savings with compound interest up to 30% — no blockchain fees |
| **Yearn.finance** | On-chain staking for Ethereum, Optimism, Fantom assets via third-party dApp |

## Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Savings on homepage">Open the **Savings** section.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose a savings plan">Scroll horizontally to select a plan. Choose by network and asset type.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Earn Now">Select the plan you want.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter deposit amount">Input how much you want to deposit.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Tap **Confirm** and wait for the transaction to process.</Step>
</Steps>

## Withdraw

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Savings">Go to the **Savings** section and select your active plan.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Withdraw">Enter the amount to withdraw.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Confirm the withdrawal. Funds return to your wallet.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For Yearn.finance savings, you receive yTokens representing your stake. These are burned when you withdraw and your original assets plus yield are returned.
</Note>
