---
title: "Saving in Fizen"
---


There are currently **2** savings services: **Fizen** and **Yearn.finance**.

* With the Fizen savings plan, we offer FiPoint loyalty point savings with compound interest of up to 30%, without any blockchain transaction fees.
* With savings plan on Yearn.finance, we link to Dapp third party and allow you to stake on-chain assets on Ethereum, Optimisticm, Fantom. You will receive yToken which represents the stake in yVault that you participates in. yvToken is minted when you deposit and is burned when you withdraw assets on vVault. For example: When depositing WETH, you will receive yvWETH. Simply by holding yToken, you will earn rewards from Yearn.finance.

#### 1. Deposit

**Step 1**: Tap **Savings** on **Homepage**

![](https://wallet-faqs.fizen.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2440689302-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252Fd4p506hiq9TbgbepkLcb%252Fuploads%252FaM4NpZbRur8rCM0KfFOk%252F7.Saving%2520%28Ti%25E1%25BA%25BFt%2520ki%25E1%25BB%2587m%29%25201.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Da748509d-78b3-45c0-b5ec-aec44f17d27e\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=414901bc\&sv=2)

**Step 2**: Scroll horizontally among pigs to choose a savings plan. The savings plan appears based on the blockchain network, please be sure to choose Ethereum, Optimistic, or Fantom if you wanna invest assets on Yearn.finance and any networks if you wanna invest FiPoint on Fizen.

You may need to scroll more to choose assets.

![](https://wallet-faqs.fizen.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2440689302-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252Fd4p506hiq9TbgbepkLcb%252Fuploads%252FpeiXAltuBD5b07GB7QCE%252F7.Saving%2520%28Ti%25E1%25BA%25BFt%2520ki%25E1%25BB%2587m%29%25202.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D6a0a2c99-324f-4c28-b7d2-3088f8421306\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=33885b0b\&sv=2)

**Step 3**: Select **Earn now** button

**Step 4**: You will be prompted to the Deposit screen. Input deposit amount.

![](https://wallet-faqs.fizen.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F2440689302-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252Fd4p506hiq9TbgbepkLcb%252Fuploads%252FhjSCVf4OGjJjbXTQWzQY%252F7.Saving%2520%28Ti%25E1%25BA%25BFt%2520ki%25E1%25BB%2587m%29%25203.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3De4bca915-b6e9-481a-bfac-24733d6558f2\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=8604626f\&sv=2)

**Step 5**: Select **Confirm**

**Step 6**: Wait for transaction to process. You will be notified once the deposit is completed.
