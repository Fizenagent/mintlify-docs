---
title: "Buy a Gift Card"
---

Shop at 2,500+ global brands, send gift cards to friends, or use them for travel. Earn FiPoints with every purchase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Gift Card section">From the Fizen Super App home screen, select **Gift Card**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose country and brand">Select your country and search for the brand you want.</Step>
  <Step title="Select amount and payment token">Choose the gift card value and select your payment token (USDT, BNB, FiGift, etc.).</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Buy Now">Confirm the purchase. Your gift card is ready instantly.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Gift cards are **one-time use only**.
</Note>
