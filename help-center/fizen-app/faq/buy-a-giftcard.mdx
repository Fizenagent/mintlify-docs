<summary>Use/ Redeem a gift card</summary>

To use the **purchased Gift Card**, please follow 1 of the 2 following options:

**Option 1:**

* On the **main screen** of Fizen Super App > Select **Gift Card**
* Select **Gift**
* Select **Gift Card**

**Option 2:**

* In the **Gift Purchase History section** > Select the **completed Gift transaction** to view detailed information
* **Click** on **the Gift Card** display section at the bottom

After accessing the Gift card, **the card** will be displayed as:

⇒ **QR Code**

⇒ **Code/Number of the card**

⇒ **Validity period**

⇒ **Expiry date**

⇒ **Hotline from Partner Brand**

<div align="center"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2F9EwOF6nObrNlTRbtMXwp%2Fz6656032278326_7816af2ff73a9a2458adbf6d7f378643.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=5af4bc9d-48a5-4bbe-aaac-83696436701a" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**To use the card, you can choose one of the following two ways:**

🔹 _**Scan the code directly:**_\
Display the **QR code** or **code/number** of the card for the store staff to **scan** the code at the checkout counter.

🔹 _**Manually enter in the application:**_\
With some application platforms such as **Grab**, **Shopee**,... you need to **copy** the **card number** and **paste** it **into the payment section** to be able to use it.

**❗ Important note:**\
All Gift Cards in Fizen are issued by **Partner Brands**. Please read **the Terms and Conditions** attached to each card carefully **before using**, as each brand will have **different policies** and **usage limits**.
