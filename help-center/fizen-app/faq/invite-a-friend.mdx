---

Invite friends to Fizen. You and your friend each get \$10 of eSIM credit when they enter your code, plus FiPoints and commission. Promotional credit, not cash.

<Tip>
  Send them the [Fizen App](https://fizen.io/app?accessCode=fizen&utm_source=web&utm_medium=fizen&utm_campaign=app_download&utm_content=docs&aid=6a8573040a0aa1256a7e5935). New users from 19 August 2026 get \$10 welcome credit automatically in the app, plus \$10 each from your personal code.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invite Friends">On the homepage, scroll down and select Invite Friends.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to share">
    - Option 1: Copy your Referral Code (you can customize it — tap Edit)
    - Option 2: Copy your Referral Link
    - Option 3: Save or share your QR Code
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend signs up">When your friend downloads Fizen and registers using your code or link, they're connected to your referral.</Step>
  <Step title="Earn rewards">You each receive \$10 of eSIM credit (you can earn this on up to 10 friends). FiPoints and commission on their transactions continue as before.</Step>
</Steps>
