title: "Invite a friend"
---


Yes, Fizen offers an exciting referral reward program.

By simply **inviting** your friends to use the Fizen Super App, you can unlock exclusive benefits and earn generous rewards. The more friends you refer, the greater the rewards — it’s our way of rewarding your support and helping grow the Fizen community.

To get your Refferal, first you need go to the **Fizen Super App** homepage, scroll down and select **Invite Friends to Earn 6,000 FiPoints**.

**You can invite in the following options:**

**Option 1:** Copy your **Referral Code**.\
(You can customize the referral code as you wish. Tap **Edit** to modify your code.)

**Option 2:** Copy the **Referral Link**.

<div align="center"><figure><img src="https://1816785625-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%2Fuploads%2FV2ZyUCQWwjYcSWXsHGUQ%2Fz6656115988803_8cb2868c9c960d82159b72b42905dedb.jpg?alt=media&#x26;token=0f303180-8945-4eb5-908f-b1f1acc60b00" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**Option 3:** Tap **Invite Friends to Earn 6,000 FiPoints** > Save or Share the **QR Code** to invite your friends to download the Fizen app.
