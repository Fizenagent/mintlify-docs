Invite friends to Fizen. You and your friend each get **\$10 eSIM credit** when they enter your code, plus FiPoints and commission.

<Tip>
  Send them [fizen.io/esim](https://fizen.io/esim?utm_source=docs_mintlify&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=esim10) first so they can claim the website \$10, then open the app with your code.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invite Friends">On the homepage, scroll down and select **Invite Friends**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to share">
    - **Option 1:** Copy your **Referral Code** (you can customize it — tap **Edit**)
    - **Option 2:** Copy your **Referral Link**
    - **Option 3:** Save or share your **QR Code**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend signs up">When your friend downloads Fizen and registers using your code or link, they're connected to your referral.</Step>
  <Step title="Earn rewards">You each receive **\$10 eSIM credit** (you can earn this on up to 10 friends). FiPoints and commission on their transactions continue as before.</Step>
</Steps>
