---
title: "Invite a Friend"
---

Invite friends to Fizen and earn FiPoints and commission rewards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invite Friends">On the homepage, scroll down and select **Invite Friends to Earn 6,000 FiPoints**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to share">
    - **Option 1:** Copy your **Referral Code** (you can customize it — tap **Edit**)
    - **Option 2:** Copy your **Referral Link**
    - **Option 3:** Save or share your **QR Code**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friend signs up">When your friend downloads Fizen and registers using your code or link, they're connected to your referral.</Step>
  <Step title="Earn rewards">You receive FiPoints and commission on their transactions automatically.</Step>
</Steps>
