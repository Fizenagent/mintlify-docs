---
title: "Top Up Phone"
---

<Note>
  Currently supports **prepaid mobile** top-ups only. For postpaid numbers, use the **Gift Card** feature instead.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Phone & Data">From the homepage, select **Phone & Data**.</Step>
  <Step title="Select country, enter phone number, choose network">Fill in your country, mobile number, and network provider.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose top-up amount and payment token">Select the amount and pay with USDT, BNB, or FiGift.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Pay">Confirm the payment. Top-up is credited to your mobile balance automatically.</Step>
</Steps>
