---
title: "Book a flight"
---


Step 1: Click on the Search section of Where can we take you to

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532703744962_51732a05966a97cd5130faec18b7caa6.jpg" alt="" width="255"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: In the Fly from section, enter the departure point

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532704661604_691cd044d5848e11db277aab51bfdc4a.jpg" alt="" width="254"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: In the Fly to section, enter the destination

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532705434558_18e3742c6a5d546691b231a075768d3d.jpg" alt="" width="252"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Note: To search for the departure point and destination, you need to type the full name of the province you want to go to, or the abbreviation of the airport you want to search for
{% endhint %}

Step 4: Select the flight type, One-way or Round Trip

* For One-way, select the departure date

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532706543988_d147c2d2cf65200df291e9b1d1cb6fd6.jpg" alt="" width="254"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* For Round Trip, select the departure date and return date

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532706908164_f54901660c7554693394e99742729578.jpg" alt="" width="255"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Passenger number information, including:

* Number of Adults
* Number of Children
* Number of Babies

Then click Find Flights

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532708492151_04bcf4387e5155ab3166c262b38af3e8.jpg" alt="" width="253"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 6: Select the flight by Airline - Flight time - Ticket price, click Select to select the flight you want

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532712270517_2c78f5e07b7c0ffea28c288258b5af0c.jpg" alt="" width="233"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 7: Fill in the passenger information. In this section, please fill in the correct information of the person booking the ticket, because the ticket information will be sent via email.

* Last name
* Gender
* Email address
* Phone number
* Passenger information

Please fill in the complete information of each passenger

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532712356709_49392ee08b55e5ce6b7432d617c19c8d.jpg" alt="" width="250"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 8: In the Service Selection section, select Baggage & Choose your seat if you want

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Choose seat.png" alt="" width="232"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 9: Check all the information again and Click Swipe to Payment in the payment section

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Payment.png" alt="" width="233"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Wait for the transaction to complete. After completing the transaction, the flight ticket information will be sent to your Email by Fizen, please check your email.

If you encounter any problems during the payment process, do not hesitate to contact the Fizen team immediately for quick support.
