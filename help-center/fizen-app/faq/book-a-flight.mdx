---
title: "Book a Flight"
---

Book flights directly from the Fizen Super App using crypto.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Travel → Flights">From the homepage, go to **Travel** and select **Flights**.</Step>
  <Step title="Search route">Tap **Where can we take you** → enter departure and destination.

  <Note>Type the full province name or airport abbreviation for accurate search results.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select flight type">Choose **One-way** or **Round Trip** and set your travel dates.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter passenger information">Fill in the number of adults and children.</Step>
  <Step title="Browse and select flight">Review available flights and select the one that fits your schedule.</Step>
  <Step title="Complete booking">Enter passenger details and pay with crypto.</Step>
</Steps>
