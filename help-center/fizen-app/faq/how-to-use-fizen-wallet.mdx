# How to use Fizen wallet?

> **⚠️ NOTES:**
>
> ⇒ When selecting an image from the Gallery, ensure you grant access to all photos.
> \
>
> \
> ⇒ For all three features—Scan, Receive, and Send—ensure you check your network connection before transferring tokens, and confirm that the sender’s and receiver’s networks are the same.

### 1. How to Scan for Payments? <a href="#id-1.-how-to-scan-for-payments" id="id-1.-how-to-scan-for-payments"></a>

With the Scan feature, you can send tokens (e.g., BNB, USDT) or fiat to others. Step 1: From the app’s main screen, select Scan to Pay.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (147).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Scan the QR code directly or select an image from the Gallery to send tokens or fiat (VND, Philippines Pesos).

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252Fx5TEgMsv2dJasCG1Loun%252Fz6655761594022_8156c801daee0b412ad9f974a9bed902.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D22ebf458-bcac-435c-bbca-5889ddc77a7e&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=7c8ef645&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

Step 3: Enter the amount of tokens or VND you wish to send, then swipe to confirm the transaction.

#### 2. How to Receive my Tokens? <a href="#id-2.-how-to-receive-my-tokens" id="id-2.-how-to-receive-my-tokens"></a>

Step 1: From the main screen, select Receive.

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FhSLBxLQwwUBsePakNONp%252Fz6655777509779_35060313828f41687d07c436401f561b.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dfa54d8dd-e1ec-411b-9dfc-85b6d0900f6c&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=de863381&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

Step 2: Tap Copy to copy your wallet address, or tap Share and select Save Image.

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FSNjhyFriNlKZFwEe8f8d%252Fz6655778133949_49be5141854abcf1dcaa907b9418233d.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D605cb3b5-a6d0-4819-9838-e1d2e7620fe2&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=c92ac8f&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

Step 3: Send your wallet address to the person sending you tokens.

#### 3. How to Send Tokens to another? <a href="#id-3.-how-to-send-tokens-to-another" id="id-3.-how-to-send-tokens-to-another"></a>

With the Send feature, you can transfer tokens from your wallet to another person’s wallet address.

Step 1: From the main screen, select Send.

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FI2elWnJ1YviGPoEUQXL7%252Fz6655786971140_cccd71e422991c7f82766aaef375caf4.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Ddfdc59d3-b28d-4e2a-9618-fcdba3fe7798&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=5599532d&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

Step 2: Copy the recipient’s wallet address and paste it into the Recipient Address field.

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FSTToKSFeIjmjJZAzvKgt%252Fz6655791676214_a4bc78d41c9fe9c7e48ae79ce79b9cfc.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D29dd6fc7-d9b6-4c8d-bf91-9381beb3f45a&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=2b3675c&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

Step 3: Select the token you want to send (e.g., USDT, BNB, ETH) > Enter the amount to send.

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252F75sIe39Uqo9auj7tzvnd%252Fz6655791688726_064ab1c609f136e0b536627f80097ea9.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dd7f4b7c1-2676-48ca-90ee-083995043afa&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=4acf4345&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

Step 4: Swipe to proceed and complete the transaction.
