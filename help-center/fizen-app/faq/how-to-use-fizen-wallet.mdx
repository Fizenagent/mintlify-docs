---
title: "How to Use Fizen Wallet"
---

<Note>
  Always check your network connection before transferring. Make sure sender and receiver are on the same network.
</Note>

## Scan to Pay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Scan to Pay on the homepage">Select the **Scan** button from the main screen.</Step>
  <Step title="Scan QR code or upload from gallery">Point camera at QR code, or select an image from your photo library.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and confirm">Input the amount, then swipe to confirm the transaction.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Receive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Receive">Find **Receive** in your wallet.</Step>
  <Step title="Select token and network">Choose the correct token and network. Share your QR code or wallet address with the sender.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Send

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Send">Open **Send** from the wallet screen.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter recipient address">Paste or scan the recipient's wallet address.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and select token">Choose the token and amount to send.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Review details and swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always double-check the recipient address before confirming. Crypto transactions are irreversible.
</Warning>
