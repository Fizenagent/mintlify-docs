title: "Purchase and activate an eSIM?"
---


{% hint style="warning" %}
**Note:**

To use an international eSIM, please ensure the following conditions are met:

* **Activate your eSIM before departure:** It’s recommended to activate your eSIM before leaving your home country to ensure you have network access immediately upon arrival, making your trip more convenient and seamless.
* **Device compatibility:** Not all devices support eSIM. You can check by going to:\
  **Settings > Cellular/Mobile > Add eSIM**.\
  If this option appears, your device is compatible with eSIM.
{% endhint %}

<details>

<summary>How to purchase an eSIM?</summary>

**Step 1:** On the **Home screen** of the **Fizen Super App**, select the **eSIM** section.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_0449.PNG" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:** Enter the **country name**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_0450.PNG" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** Choose a suitable eSIM package based on the following information:

* **Validity period**
* **Amount of data (MB/GB per day)**
* **Coverage area**

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_0451.PNG" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4:** Select your desired **data allowance** and **validity duration** > Tap **Buy Now** and **confirm the transaction**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_0452.PNG" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

After the transaction is completed, the system will display full eSIM information along with a **QR code** that you can use.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How to activate an eSIM?</summary>

#### **Part 1: Adding an eSIM to Your Phone** <a href="#part-1-adding-an-esim-to-your-phone" id="part-1-adding-an-esim-to-your-phone"></a>

**Step 1:** On your phone, go to **Settings** > tap **Mobile/Cellular**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532659095025_0cdbc76134b2fbda8949bc31790c86a3.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:** Look for the option to **Add eSIM** > choose **Use QR Code**, then add your eSIM using one of the two methods below:

* **Method 1:** Use your phone’s camera to **scan the eSIM QR code directly**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532659084965_41361a0f32ab8ba3524d594678c19f3b.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*   **Method 2:** Tap **Enter Details Manually**. For this method, you will need to manually enter the activation information provided by the carrier:

    * Open the **Fizen Super App** > copy the **SM-DP+ Address** > return to **Settings** and paste it.
    * Then go back to the **Fizen Super App** > copy the **Activation Code** > return to **Settings** and paste it.



<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/IMG_0459.PNG" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3:** Select **Travel** > continue through all setup prompts, including:

* **Activate eSIM**
* **Default Line**
* **iMessage & FaceTime**
* **Mobile Data**

Then tap **Continue** or **Confirm**.

**Step 4:** Tap **Set Up Later** when prompted > then proceed through all steps under **Update Preferred Line for Contacts**.

#### **Part 2: Activating the eSIM** <a href="#part-2-activating-the-esim" id="part-2-activating-the-esim"></a>

**Step 1:** On your phone, go to **Settings** > tap **Mobile/Cellular**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532659095025_0cdbc76134b2fbda8949bc31790c86a3 (1).jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:** Configure other eSIM settings on your device (e.g., name, label, etc.).

**Step 3:** Activate the eSIM by enabling **Data Roaming** and **Turn On This Line**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/z7532659097562_abfa674334fdd854fe991ecce8d392cc.jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary>Transaction status notes</summary>

There are **four transaction status types** you should know:

After completing an eSIM purchase transaction, you can review the transaction details on the confirmation screen. No further action is required.

1. **Processing Transaction**: The transaction is currently being processed. Please wait until it is completed, and your purchased eSIM becomes available.
2. **Completed Transaction**: The transaction has been successfully completed. You can open the order to view your purchased eSIM.
3. **Refunded Transaction**: The transaction could not be completed for various reasons, such as the eSIM being out of stock or an issue with the eSIM provider. The amount you paid will be automatically refunded to your wallet. To check the refunded amount, return to the main screen and tap the **History icon**.
4. **Failed Transaction**: The transaction was canceled due to an error, possibly caused by user action or a network issue on your device. In this case, please exit the Fizen app temporarily, check your internet connection, reopen the app, and initiate a new transaction.
