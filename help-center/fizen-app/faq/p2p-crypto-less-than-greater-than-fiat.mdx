## How to buy/sell crypto on the Fizen Super App?

With the **P2P (peer-to-peer)** feature, you can **buy cryptocurrencies** (e.g.,USDT, BNB, ETH) **using fiat currency** (e.g., EUR, USD, VND), and **sell cryptocurrencies to receive fiat**. Below is a step-by-step guide to buying and selling crypto using Fizen P2P.

To begin using this feature, go to the **Homepage** of the **Fizen Super App** > Tap on **Fizen P2P**, then follow these steps:

#### 1. Buy crypto

**Step 1:** In the **Fizen P2P** section > Select **Buy**.

If you encounter any issues while tracking your order, please use the **Support button** to contact **Fizen Customer Service** for immediate assistance.

The transaction is expected to be completed **within 1–10 minutes** after you finish the transfer. However, completion time may vary depending on your bank's processing speed. If you encounter any issues while tracking your order, please use the **Support button** to contact **Fizen Customer Service** for immediate assistance.

After you create a **P2P** order, the **order status** will be displayed as follows:

**Order ID**: This is the ID number to **classify** and store your order. If you have any problems with your order, please **copy the order ID**, along with the transfer image (for purchase orders) and **send** it to Customer Service for support

**Receive money status**: Estimated transaction completion time is 1-10 minutes, depending on the bank. Transaction completion time may vary depending on the bank's receiving & processing process. The transaction status transition process includes:

_**For P2P purchase orders**: Create order ⇒ Received VND ⇒ Sent Crypto_

_**For P2P sale orders**: Create order ⇒ Received Crypto ⇒ Sent Fiat_

**Detailed information**, including:

* **Receiving address**
* **Transaction creation time**
* **Purchase quantity/Sell quantity**
* **Network**
