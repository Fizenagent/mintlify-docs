---
title: "P2P Crypto ↔ Fiat"
---

Buy crypto with fiat or sell crypto for fiat — directly in the Fizen app via P2P.

## Buy crypto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fizen P2P">Homepage → **Fizen P2P** → tap **Buy**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter fiat amount">Input how much local currency you want to spend.</Step>
  <Step title="Agree to Terms and confirm">Accept Terms & Conditions and swipe to confirm.</Step>
  <Step title="Complete the bank transfer">
    **IMPORTANT:** Copy and paste the following exactly as shown — do not modify:
    - Bank name
    - Bank account number
    - Transfer message

    Any error may result in loss of funds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sell crypto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Fizen P2P">Homepage → **Fizen P2P** → tap **Sell**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter crypto amount">Input how much crypto you want to sell.</Step>
  <Step title="Select payout method">Choose your bank account for fiat payout.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Swipe to confirm. Fiat is credited after the transaction is verified.</Step>
</Steps>
