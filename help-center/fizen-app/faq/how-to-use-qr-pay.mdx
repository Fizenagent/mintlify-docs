<Note>
  QR Pay is available in **Vietnam** and the **Philippines**, for travellers and foreigners. More regions coming soon.
  It pays VietQR bank codes, VNPay, MoMo and ZaloPay codes in Vietnam, and QR Ph codes in the Philippines.
</Note>

## How it works

Your **Fizen wallet** is non-custodial: you hold the keys. To pay, you deposit the USDT you plan to spend from your wallet into **QR Pay**, then scan the shop's QR code and pay. The merchant receives local currency.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or import your Fizen wallet">
    Download the Fizen app and create a new wallet, or import an existing one with your seed phrase. See [Set Up Wallet](/help-center/fizen-app/wallet-and-account/set-up-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get USDT on Solana or BNB Chain into your wallet">
    Tap **Receive**, choose **USDT** and the **Solana** or **BNB Chain** network, and send USDT to that address. If your USDT is on another network, move it with **Swap & Bridge** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit from your wallet into QR Pay">
    Open **QR Pay**, complete identity verification (KYC), then tap **Deposit**, enter the USDT amount and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and pay">
    Tap **Scan**, point the camera at the merchant's QR code, check the merchant name and the amount in local currency, then swipe to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you deposit

<Warning>
  **QR Pay is funded only with USDT on Solana or BNB Chain.**

  - No bank account link, no card top-up, and no VND or PHP deposit.
  - Not USDC, not BTC, and not USDT on Tron or Ethereum.
  - USDT on another network, or another token, **cannot be deposited** into QR Pay.

  If your USDT is on another network, use **Swap & Bridge** in the app to move it to Solana or BNB Chain first.
</Warning>

<Info>
  QR Pay is for **cross-border use only**: you cannot use it to pay merchants in your home country, so residents of Vietnam and the Philippines cannot use it there. KYC applies per wallet and per device: each wallet completes KYC, and using QR Pay on a new device requires KYC again. Use of QR Pay is governed by the [Fizen QR Pay Terms](https://docs.fizen.io/fizen-qr-pay). The Fizen wallet is self-custody; USDT you deposit into QR Pay is governed by the QR Pay Terms, so only move in what you plan to spend.
</Info>

## Deposit into QR Pay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open QR Pay">On the homepage, select **QR Pay**.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Deposit">Select the **Deposit** option.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter USDT amount">Enter how much USDT to move from your Fizen wallet, from USDT on **Solana** or **BNB Chain** only.</Step>
  <Step title="Agree to Terms and confirm">Accept the Terms & Conditions, then swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Pay a merchant via QR

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Scan in QR Pay">Open QR Pay and tap **Scan**.</Step>
  <Step title="Scan the merchant QR code">Point the camera at the merchant's QR code.</Step>
  <Step title="Check, enter the amount and confirm">Check the merchant name, enter the amount if the code does not include it, check the amount in local currency, then swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep the payment message plain: letters, numbers and spaces only. Many banks reject a transfer whose message contains special characters such as a hyphen, slash, dot or equals sign. The in-app keyboard has none of these.
</Tip>

## Transfer between QR Pay accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Transfer">Open QR Pay and select **Transfer**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter recipient">Enter the recipient's phone number or QR Pay ID.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and confirm">Enter the amount and swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Up to **US$800** per payment.
- Up to **US$1,000** per week per user.
- No daily limit.
- In **Vietnam**, QR Pay pays QR codes of Vietnamese account holders. Codes linked to an account held by a foreigner will not go through.

## Why a payment can fail

- **The receiving bank is under maintenance or having a problem.** If the payment shows as failed, try again later or contact support.
- **The payment is still processing.** Do not pay again. Wait for the final status so you do not pay twice.
- **In Vietnam, the account belongs to a foreigner.** Ask the merchant for a code linked to a Vietnamese account.
- **The QR code is old or expired.** Ask the merchant for a new one.
- **The payment message has special characters.** Ask the merchant for a plain code.
- **The amount is over your limit.**
