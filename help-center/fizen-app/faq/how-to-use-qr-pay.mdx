# How to use QR Pay?

> **⚠️ NOTES:**
>
> * QR Pay is currently available in Vietnam, Philippines. Support for other regions will be launched soon.
> * You can scan **VietQR, Momo, and ZaloPay** codes.

#### 1. How to Deposit into Your QR Pay Balance 

**Step 1:** On the Fizen Super App home screen, select **QR Pay**


**Step 2:** Tap Deposit


**Step 3:** Enter the amount of **USDT** you want to deposit


**Step 4:** Agree to Fizen’s **Terms & Conditions > Swipe to confirm**


***

#### **2.** How to Scan QR Codes Using QR Pay 

**Step 1:** In the **QR Pay** section, select **Scan to Spend**


**Step 2: Point the camera** at the QR code to scan, or **select** a QR code image from your photo gallery

> _Note: You should allow the app to access your camera and photo gallery_


**Step 3:** Enter the amount in **VND** and add a **message**


**Step 4:** **Swipe to confirm**

#### 3. How to Transfer USDT 

The "Send" section includes two features: **Withdraw USDT to your Fizen wallet**, or **Transfer USDT to another QR Pay account**.

**3. 1. Withdraw USDT to Your Fizen Wallet**

**Step 1:** Go to **QR Pay** > Select **Withdraw to your Fizen wallet**


**Step 2: Enter** the amount in **USDT > Swipe to Confirm**


**3.2. Transfer USDT to Another QR Pay Account**

**Step 1:** Go to **QR Pay** > Select **Transfer to another QR Pay account**


**Step 2:** Enter the **amount in USDT**, the **recipient’s wallet address** (Binance Smart Chain network), and a **message** > **Swipe to confirm**


After completing a QR Pay transaction, you can review the transaction details on the confirmation screen. No further action is required.

**Transaction History Screen Displays the Following Information:**


**After Scanning a QR Code**

* **Recipient Account Information**, including:
  * Recipient Name
  * Bank Account Number
  * Bank Name
* **Amount Sent**
* **Message**
* **Date & Time of Transaction Creation**
* **Transaction ID**

***


**After Transferring to Another QR Pay Account:**

1. **QR Pay Address of Sender and Recipient**
2. **Amount Sent**
3. **Date & Time of Transaction Creation**
4. **Transaction ID**

***
