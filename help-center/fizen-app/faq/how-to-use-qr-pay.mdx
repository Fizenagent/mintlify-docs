# How to use QR Pay?

> **⚠️ NOTES:**
>
> * QR Pay is currently available in Vietnam, Philippines. Support for other regions will be launched soon.
> * You can scan **VietQR, Momo, and ZaloPay** codes.

#### **2.** How to Scan QR Codes Using QR Pay <a href="#id-2.-how-to-scan-qr-codes-using-qr-pay" id="id-2.-how-to-scan-qr-codes-using-qr-pay"></a>

**Step 1:** In the **QR Pay** section, select **Scan to Spend**

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FSDcC3HVfXO9fwCfdMljF%252Fz6655840149156_f7c9edbeb20c24126164b3f43c81311f.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D8122947e-5318-4087-8551-416742533f14&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=b890dbbc&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

**Step 2: Point the camera** at the QR code to scan, or **select** a QR code image from your photo gallery

> _Note: You should allow the app to access your camera and photo gallery_

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FQTaq8kGlZNAZTMnUlllf%252Fz6655845758663_ffa671b11f0110a8841a7e1eef20c679.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D431e08ac-645e-483f-bc3b-4dc22509592b&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=5c1d8164&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

**Step 3:** Enter the amount in **VND** and add a **message**

#### 3. How to Transfer USDT <a href="#id-3.-how-to-send-usdt" id="id-3.-how-to-send-usdt"></a>

The "Send" section includes two features: **Withdraw USDT to your Fizen wallet**, or **Transfer USDT to another QR Pay account**.

**3. 1. Withdraw USDT to Your Fizen Wallet**

**Step 1:** Go to **QR Pay** > Select **Withdraw to your Fizen wallet**

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252Fu9QPi5mW4q7gf1CsA3k7%252Fz6655859750449_19c0939de5b11d4f59660fcacbdc8d79.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dfcea79a6-bad3-4fdf-8efa-989f370c76cf&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=63af2090&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

**Step 2: Enter** the amount in **USDT > Swipe to Confirm**

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FI1HlWZHqPK2p5OYguXVZ%252Fz6655859754989_7b9b050f25140ed88429301675037acf.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dbd9890dd-0b3a-45a6-9858-d342a28b8f6a&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=b6110631&#x26;sv=2" alt=""><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252FpMXYzQ99G1nRM49PJjLw%252Fz6655859766457_40f30c72353ea472921ad09e0559e4aa.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D6de85af1-f619-4272-ad3b-7ba0260883d3&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=ce8c04d3&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>

**3.2. Transfer USDT to Another QR Pay Account**

**Step 1:** Go to **QR Pay** > Select **Transfer to another QR Pay account**

<p align="center"><img src="https://fizen-super-app.gitbook.io/fizen.io.faq/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F1816785625-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FdmjnJs1cMBTQtMIP8bNc%252Fuploads%252F6hrQ7wttgpkxEp3KYbdi%252F1.jpg%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Daa54c9bc-c2c5-4756-bdf0-4ae6f0bca0b6&#x26;width=768&#x26;dpr=4&#x26;quality=100&#x26;sign=512f3e6e&#x26;sv=2" alt=""></p>
