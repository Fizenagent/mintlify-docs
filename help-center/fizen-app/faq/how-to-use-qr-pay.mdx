---
title: "How to Use QR Pay"
---

<Note>
  QR Pay is currently available in **Vietnam** and **Philippines**. More regions coming soon.
  Supports VietQR, Momo, and ZaloPay codes.
</Note>

## Deposit into QR Pay balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open QR Pay">On the homepage, select **QR Pay**.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Deposit">Select the **Deposit** option.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter USDT amount">Input how much USDT you want to deposit.</Step>
  <Step title="Agree to Terms and confirm">Accept Terms & Conditions, then swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Pay a merchant via QR

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Scan in QR Pay">Open QR Pay → tap **Scan**.</Step>
  <Step title="Scan merchant QR code">Point camera at the merchant's QR code.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and confirm">Input the payment amount and swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Transfer between QR Pay accounts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Transfer">Open QR Pay → select **Transfer**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter recipient">Input the recipient's phone number or QR Pay ID.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and confirm">Specify amount and swipe to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
