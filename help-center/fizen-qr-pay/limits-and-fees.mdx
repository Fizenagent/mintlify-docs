## Limits

| Limit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Per payment | US$800 |
| Per week, per user | US$1,000 |

There is no limit on the number of payments in a day. Transfers between QR Pay accounts count towards the same limits.

<Tip>
  Paying for something above US$800, such as a hotel bill? Ask whether the merchant can split it into separate QR codes, or pay the balance another way.
</Tip>

---

## Fees

Any fee is shown in the app **before you confirm**, so you always see the total before you swipe. Fees depend on the payment, which is why we do not quote a single fixed number here.

---

## Referral fee vouchers

Inviting friends earns fee vouchers that reduce what you pay in fees:

- Up to **US$50** in fee vouchers in total.
- Applied at a maximum of **US$0.20 per payment**.

<Note>
  The old 5% bonus for holding a QR Pay balance has ended. A new programme is on the way and will be announced in the app.
</Note>
