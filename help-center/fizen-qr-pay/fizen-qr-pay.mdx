QR Pay lets you pay local QR codes while you travel, straight from the USDT you already hold. No local bank account, no cash, no leftover foreign currency at the end of the trip.

<Note>
  QR Pay is live in **Vietnam** and the **Philippines**, for travellers and foreigners, and is extending to Latin America.
  It is funded only with **USDT on Solana or BNB Chain**.
</Note>

## Good to know before you start

- **Who it is for.** In Vietnam and the Philippines, QR Pay is for visitors and foreigners. Residents cannot use it to pay at home, because QR Pay is for cross border use. In Latin America, where it is coming next, it will be open to everyone including residents.
- **Your wallet and QR Pay are different things.** The Fizen wallet is self custody and you hold the keys. USDT you move into QR Pay is governed by the [Fizen QR Pay Terms](/fizen-qr-pay), so only move in what you plan to spend.
- **Funding.** USDT on Solana or BNB Chain only. No bank account, no card, no local currency.
