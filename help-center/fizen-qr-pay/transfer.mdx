---
title: "Transfer Between QR Pay Accounts"
description: "Send part of your QR Pay balance to another QR Pay user with their phone number or QR Pay ID."
---

You can move balance from your QR Pay account to another QR Pay user, which is handy for splitting a bill or paying back a travel companion.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Transfer">
    Open QR Pay and select **Transfer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the recipient">
    Enter their **phone number** or **QR Pay ID**. Check it with them before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and confirm">
    Enter the amount, review what is shown, then swipe to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Both sides need a QR Pay account with [identity verification](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/kyc) completed. Transfers count towards your [limits](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/limits-and-fees) in the same way payments do.
</Info>

<Note>
  Sending USDT to someone outside QR Pay is a different job. Use the wallet's own send function, or **Send Money** in the app, rather than QR Pay.
</Note>
