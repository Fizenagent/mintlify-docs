---
title: "Why a Payment Fails"
description: "The six common reasons a QR Pay payment does not go through, and what to do about each one."
---

Most failed payments come down to one of six things. Work down the list in order.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The receiving bank is under maintenance or having a problem">
    Nothing is wrong on your side. If the payment shows as failed, try again a little later, or contact support in the app and we will check the status for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The payment is still processing">
    **Do not pay again.** Wait for the final status first, otherwise the merchant may be paid twice. If it stays in processing for a long time, contact support rather than retrying.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="In Vietnam, the account belongs to a foreigner">
    In Vietnam, QR Pay pays QR codes of Vietnamese account holders. Ask the merchant for a different code. This rule does not apply in the Philippines.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The QR code is old or expired">
    Some codes are generated per transaction and stop working after a while. Ask the merchant to show you a fresh code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The payment message contains special characters">
    Many banks reject a transfer whose message has characters outside letters, numbers and spaces, such as a hyphen, slash, dot or equals sign. The in app keyboard has none of these, so if the merchant's code carries such a message, ask for a plain code.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The amount is over your limit">
    QR Pay allows US$800 per payment and US$1,000 per week per user. See [Limits and Fees](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/limits-and-fees).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Deposit did not arrive

If your QR Pay balance did not change after a deposit, check the network you sent from. QR Pay accepts **USDT on Solana or BNB Chain** only. USDT on another network, or another token, cannot be deposited into QR Pay. Move it to Solana or BNB Chain with **Swap & Bridge** first, then deposit again. See [Deposit into QR Pay](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/deposit).

<Info>
  Still stuck? Open the **in app live chat** with the payment time, the amount and a screenshot. A real person will look into it.
</Info>
