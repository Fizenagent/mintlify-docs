Once you have a QR Pay balance, paying is the same everywhere: scan, check, confirm. The merchant receives local currency.

## Pay a merchant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Scan in QR Pay">
    Open QR Pay and tap **Scan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the merchant's QR code">
    Point the camera at the code. A printed sticker at the counter works the same as a code on a screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    Check the merchant name, then enter the amount if the code does not already include it. The app shows the amount in local currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swipe to confirm">
    Any fee appears before you confirm, so you always see the total first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Which QR codes work

| Country | Codes accepted |
|---|---|
| Vietnam | VietQR bank codes, VNPay, MoMo, ZaloPay |
| Philippines | QR Ph |

<Warning>
  **In Vietnam only:** QR Pay pays QR codes belonging to Vietnamese account holders. A code linked to an account held by a foreigner will not go through, so ask the merchant for a different code. This rule does not apply in the Philippines.
</Warning>

## Keep the payment message plain

Many banks reject a transfer whose message contains characters outside letters, numbers and spaces, such as a hyphen, a slash, a dot or an equals sign. When that happens the payment fails.

- The in app message keyboard has no special characters, so anything you type is already safe.
- If the merchant's QR code carries a message with special characters in it, ask them for a plain code.

<Tip>
  If a payment shows as still processing, do not pay again. Wait for the final status so you do not pay twice. More in [Why a Payment Fails](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/why-a-payment-fails).
</Tip>
