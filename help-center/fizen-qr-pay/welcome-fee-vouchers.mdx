---
title: "US$10 Welcome Fee Vouchers"
description: "New Fizen users get US$10 in QR Pay fee vouchers. How to claim them on the home screen and apply one on every payment."
---

**New Fizen app users only** get a **US$10 QR Pay gift**, paid as fee vouchers. Existing accounts do not get it. Each voucher takes up to US$0.20 off the fee of one QR Pay payment. The numbers are in [Limits and Fees](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/limits-and-fees#welcome-fee-vouchers).

## Claim your vouchers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update and open the app">
    Update Fizen to the latest version and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the gift card on the home screen">
    Look for the card **Enjoy $10 QR Pay fee discount!**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Claim">
    Tap **Claim**, not Ignore. Your vouchers are added to **My vouchers**, where you can see how many are left and when each one expires.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Claiming is a one-time step, for new users only. If you are new and the card is not on your home screen after updating, contact support in the app.
</Note>

---

## Apply a voucher every time you pay

A voucher is **not applied for you**. Choose one on each payment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the merchant's QR code">
    Open QR Pay, tap **Scan to spend** and scan the code. Enter the amount if the code does not include it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a voucher on the confirm screen">
    Tap the voucher line and pick a QR Pay fee voucher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the fee, then confirm">
    The fee shown before you confirm should now be lower. Swipe to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  One voucher per payment. Use the next one on your next payment, until all of them are used or they expire.
</Tip>

---

## Before your first payment

- Your identity check (KYC) must be approved. See [KYC](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/kyc).
- Your QR Pay balance must hold **USDT on BNB Chain or Solana**. See [Deposit](/help-center/fizen-qr-pay/deposit).
- The vouchers only reduce QR Pay payment fees. They are separate from the US$10 eSIM credit in the eSIM tab and from referral fee vouchers.
