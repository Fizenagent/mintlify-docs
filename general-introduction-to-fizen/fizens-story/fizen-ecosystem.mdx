## Where We're Going

Crypto payments are still early. Most people holding USDT today still convert via P2P to spend it — paying fees, waiting, taking risks. Most merchants still don't accept crypto. That gap is exactly where Fizen operates.

But this is changing fast.

## The world we're building toward

In the next 3–5 years, we believe:

- People will receive salaries in crypto
- Savings will sit in stablecoins earning yield
- Everyday purchases — coffee, groceries, travel — will be paid with USDT as naturally as tapping a card
- Cross-border payments will take seconds, not days, and cost cents, not dollars

Fizen is building for that world **right now** — not waiting for it to arrive.
