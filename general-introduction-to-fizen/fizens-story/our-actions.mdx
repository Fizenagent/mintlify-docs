To enable businesses to accept cryptocurrency payments, we provide the Fizen Pay cryptocurrency payment gateway. It is a robust software solution for businesses to seamlessly incorporate cryptocurrencies into their payments, covering both online sales of products and services and in-person transactions at physical retail locations. Businesses have the flexibility to withdraw cryptocurrencies or convert cryptocurrencies to their local fiat currency.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Hành động ra sao 1.jpg" alt="" width="336"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For users, we offer support for both those who already possess cryptocurrency and those who don't. We provide a powerful set of tools for users to exchange various cryptocurrencies for fiat currencies in multiple countries, known as Fizen Ramp.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Hành động ra sao 2.jpg" alt="" width="363"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Most other startups in this area stop here. They either offer cryptocurrency payment solutions for businesses or solutions for users to buy and sell cryptocurrencies. However, you can see that businesses often struggle to find cryptocurrency users, and conversely, cryptocurrency users don't always know where to spend their crypto.

Therefore, Fizen acts as an intermediary, connecting users and businesses, making cryptocurrency circulate smoothly, and increasing its practical utility. One of our applications designed for this purpose is the Fizen Super App.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Hành động ra sao 3 (1).jpg" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Why is it called a "Super App"?&#x20;

Fizen provides a wide range of products and services for users within a single smartphone application. Why is it a smartphone app? We believe that no one wants to go to a café, open a computer, launch a web browser, log into Metamask, visit an e-commerce website, add items to the cart, and pay with cryptocurrency. That's just too inconvenient. In contrast, Fizen offers a user-friendly mobile app with a quick and convenient interface, catering even to those less tech-savvy. It only takes a few seconds to purchase a coffee with cryptocurrency. You can easily conduct transactions right at the point of sale! It may sound unbelievable, but you can watch this video for a demonstration:  [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLQa7Jp9mpk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WLQa7Jp9mpk)

The experience of making payments with cryptocurrency for various applications in daily life is truly fantastic. This sentiment isn't exclusive to us; many loyal Fizen users and partners feel the same way. Our partners are keen to enable their tokens for payments, and we have provided an excellent platform for this purpose.

As a result, Fizen has evolved into an ecosystem, serving as a global payment and cross-border e-commerce platform. Let's take a closer look at the Fizen ecosystem.
