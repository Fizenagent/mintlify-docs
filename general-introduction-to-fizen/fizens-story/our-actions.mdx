# Our Actions

Most crypto startups stop at one side of the problem — either a payment gateway for merchants, or a wallet for users. But businesses can't find crypto users, and users don't know where to spend.

**Fizen connects both sides.**

## What we've built

**Fizen Pay** — a crypto payment gateway for businesses. Accept USDT online or in-store. Withdraw crypto or convert to local fiat.

**Fizen Super App** — the spending layer for users. Buy/sell crypto, send money internationally, pay by QR, get eSIM, top up phone, buy gift cards, trade tokenized U.S. stocks. Everything in one mobile app.

**Fizen Card** — a USDT-funded Visa card. Load USDT, spend at 150M+ Visa merchants worldwide. Up to 200 cards per account. Works with Google Pay and Apple Pay.

**Fizen QR Pay** — scan to pay with USDT at participating merchants, especially across Southeast Asia.

## What this creates

By connecting merchants who accept crypto with users who hold crypto, Fizen makes USDT circulate in real life — not just sit on exchanges.

This is how crypto becomes useful. Not through hype. Through actual daily spending.
