## Why one app

Most crypto apps do one thing. Fizen does everything — because real life doesn't happen in silos.

You travel → you need eSIM, local payments, and a card that works everywhere.
You freelance → you receive crypto, convert, send, and spend without losing 3% every step.
You invest → you trade US stocks from your USDT balance without opening a brokerage account.

One app. One balance. No friction.
