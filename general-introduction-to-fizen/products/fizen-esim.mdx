## What is Fizen eSIM?

An eSIM is a digital SIM built into your phone — no plastic card, nothing to swap. Fizen eSIM lets you install a local data plan for your destination before you fly, so mobile data works the moment you land. It runs alongside your regular SIM: your number stays active for calls and texts, while the eSIM carries your internet at local prices instead of roaming rates.
