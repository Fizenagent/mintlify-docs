# Products

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/telegram-peer-photo-size-5-6265051228715201245-1-0-0.jpg" alt="" width="160"><figcaption><p><a href="../../teams/building-the-project/board-of-director/vinh-vu-cto-and-co-founder.md">Vinh Vũ – CTO and Co-Founder</a></p></figcaption></figure>

Fizen's technical team has many years of experience in building mobile apps, web applications, and blockchain solutions. We follow the product philosophy of Steve Jobs: when you unbox the product, you should be able to use it immediately without needing to read instructions. Everything is meticulously and carefully crafted, even the parts that users don't see. We always strive to do our best, continuously refining every detail of Fizen's products every day, every hour. We listen to our customers' needs and make continuous improvements.

Let's learn about Fizen's three main products:

1. Fizen Super App: a super application designed for global users.
2. Fizen Pay Payment Gateway: a cryptocurrency payment gateway solution for global businesses.
3. Fizen Ramp: a collection of solutions for buying and selling cryptocurrencies with fiat currency in many countries.
