Fizen Card is a USD Visa prepaid card funded directly with USDT. Load crypto, spend anywhere Visa is accepted — online, in-store, or via Google Pay and Apple Pay.
---

## How it works

**USDT → Fizen Card → Spend anywhere Visa works**
No P2P. No bank wire. No losing 2–5% on every conversion.

| Feature | Detail |

| **Card type** | Virtual (instant) + Physical (optional) |
