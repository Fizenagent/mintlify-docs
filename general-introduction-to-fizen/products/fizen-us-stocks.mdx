## What it is

A tokenized stock is a blockchain token that tracks the price of a share listed in the U.S. market. In Fizen, each token is issued by **Ondo**, which holds the underlying securities with third-party custodians. Token names are the ticker plus "on": AAPLon tracks Apple, NVDAon tracks NVIDIA, SPYon tracks the SPDR S&P 500 ETF.

The token is not the share itself. As the [Terms](/tokenized-rwa) (section 7.4) put it, it "is a tokenized representation". Your rights (dividends, voting, corporate actions, redemption) are defined by Ondo and may be less than those of a direct shareholder.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold a stablecoin in your Fizen wallet">
    Keep USDT or USDC on Ethereum in your wallet, plus a little ETH for gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a token and an amount">
    Open **U.S. Stock**, choose the token and enter what you want to spend. The app shows the rate, slippage, minimum receive and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the trade yourself">
    You confirm by signing in your own wallet. The trade runs on-chain through 1inch Fusion against liquidity providers onboarded by Ondo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the token in your wallet">
    The token settles directly to your self-custody Fizen wallet, usually within a few minutes. Selling works the same way in reverse, and you receive a stablecoin back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who it is for

- People outside the U.S. who already hold stablecoins and want exposure to well-known U.S. companies and ETFs without opening a foreign brokerage account
- Crypto users who want part of their portfolio linked to the traditional stock market, held in their own wallet
- Anyone who wants to buy small amounts, since tokens can be bought in fractions

It is **not** for money you need to keep safe or may need soon. Tokenized stocks are an investment, and their value can fall. Consider independent advice before you invest.
