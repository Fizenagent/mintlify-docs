## What it is

QR code payment is how everyday spending works across much of Asia, from street food stalls to taxis. Visitors are usually locked out of it, because the local apps want a local bank account.

QR Pay removes that barrier. You keep your money as USDT in your own Fizen wallet, move across what you plan to spend, and pay the same codes everyone else pays.

## Why travellers use it

- **No local bank account.** Nothing to open, nothing to close when you leave.
- **No cash to carry or change back.** You stop hunting for ATMs and you go home without a pile of unusable notes.
- **The rate and any fee are visible before you confirm.** No surprise on a card statement two days later.
- **It works where cards often do not.** Small shops, markets and taxis take QR codes long before they take cards.

## Where it works

| Country | Who can use it | Codes accepted |
|---|---|---|
| Vietnam | Visitors and foreigners in Vietnam | VietQR bank codes, VNPay, MoMo, ZaloPay |
| Philippines | Visitors and foreigners in the Philippines | QR Ph |
| Latin America | Coming next, open to everyone including residents | Announced at launch |

<Info>
  QR Pay is for cross border use, so you cannot pay merchants in your home country. Residents of Vietnam and the Philippines cannot use it at home, though they can use it when they travel elsewhere.
</Info>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or import your Fizen wallet">
    The wallet is self custody, so you hold the keys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold USDT on Solana or BNB Chain">
    These are the two networks QR Pay is funded from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit into QR Pay">
    Verify your identity once, then move across what you plan to spend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and pay">
    Check the merchant and the amount in local currency, then swipe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Fizen wallet is self custody. QR Pay works differently: USDT you move into QR Pay is governed by the [Fizen QR Pay Terms](/fizen-qr-pay), so only move in what you plan to spend.
</Note>
