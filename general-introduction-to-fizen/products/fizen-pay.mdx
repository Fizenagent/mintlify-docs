# Fizen Pay

### **Our Vision**

Payments should be simple, direct, and accessible.\
Crypto was born for this purpose, but the world needed infrastructure that makes it usable.

With Fizen Pay, we’re bringing **real utility** back to crypto and giving businesses a faster, cheaper, smarter way to get paid.

This is how we unlock the next wave of mass adoption.

### What Is Fizen Pay?

**Fizen Pay is an all-in-one crypto payment solution for businesses,** from restaurants and retail stores to online platforms and global merchants.

We remove the complexity of on-chain payments and give businesses:

* **Ultra-low fees** (typically <1%)
* **Instant settlement**—no 3–15 day delays like traditional card rails
* **Support for leading stablecoins** (USDT, USDC) and major chains (Solana, Ethereum, Tron, BNB Chain, etc.)
* **A seamless payment experience**—QR Pay, in-store, online checkout, and API integrations

Businesses can immediately reduce operating costs, increase margins, or convert savings into cashback, loyalty points, or product discounts for their customers.
