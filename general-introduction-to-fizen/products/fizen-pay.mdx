| 2–3% card processing fee | Typically less than 1% |

| Geographic limits | Global — anyone, anywhere |

## What you get

- **QR Pay** — generate a QR code, customer scans and pays with USDT in seconds
- **Online checkout** — embed Fizen Pay into your website or platform
- **API & Webhooks** — full developer integration for custom flows
- **Instant settlement** — funds arrive immediately, no batch delays
- **Multi-chain support** — USDT on Solana, Ethereum, Tron, BNB Chain
- **Non-custodial** — withdraw to your own wallet anytime
