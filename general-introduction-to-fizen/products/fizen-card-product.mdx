Fizen Card is a USD Visa prepaid card funded directly with USDT. Load crypto, spend anywhere Visa is accepted — online, in-store, or via Google Pay and Apple Pay.

## How it works

**USDT → Fizen Card → Spend anywhere Visa works.**

No P2P. No bank wire. No losing 2–5% on every conversion.

---

## Key features

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Network** | Visa — 150M+ merchants worldwide |
| **Funding** | USDT (Solana) |
| **Card type** | Virtual (instant) + Physical (optional) |
| **Cards per account** | Up to 200 |
| **Apple Pay / Google Pay** | ✅ Supported |
| **US BIN** | ✅ Works on Meta, Google, TikTok Ads |
| **Eligible nationalities** | Including Russian & Chinese nationals |
| **Top-up fee** | Free (network gas fee applies) |

---

## Who it's for

- **USDT holders** done with P2P conversions
- **Travelers** who want one card that works everywhere
- **Media buyers** running ad spend on global platforms
- **Freelancers** paid in crypto internationally
- **Teams & agencies** needing multiple cards from one account

<Note>
  Backed by **Tether**. Fizen Card is issued by Fizen and runs on the **Visa** global network.
</Note>
