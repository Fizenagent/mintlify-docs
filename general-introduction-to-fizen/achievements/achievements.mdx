# Achievements

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (96).png" alt="" width="170"><figcaption><p><a href="/broken/pages/eBjXBJJTpskvq1w0S07o">Trang Duong - CBO</a> and Co-Founder</p></figcaption></figure>

Fizen started in January 2022, and we spent a lot of time researching the market and building our first basic MVP product. Fizen Super App and Fizen Pay were launched in October 2022.

In the one-year period from the launch date to now (10/2022 - 10/2023), a small, highly efficient team of just 15 people, including our technical team, business development team, and marketing team, has achieved certain results, partially proving the effective and highly potential business model of Fizen.

The most important components in the Fizen ecosystem are as follows:

1. **Business clients** providing products and services: Finding, connecting, convincing, and integrating these business customers into using Fizen takes time, but it's also a barrier when competitors want to catch up with Fizen. Fizen doesn't just provide technology solutions but also helps businesses expand their operations and increase revenue.
2. **Users**: Our goal is not just about reaching a small number of people who are already familiar with cryptocurrencies but rather the billions who have not been exposed to this technology. Making users understand new technologies like Blockchain and cryptocurrencies is a significant task. Therefore, we collaborate with various brands and partners through programs and entertainment events to introduce technology to the public in a gentle, understandable, and accessible way.
3. **Brands and Partners**: For many people, blockchain projects often have negative connotations. We aim to change this perception by building Fizen into a reputable, trustworthy brand with long-term, sustainable goals that bring real value to society. Our partners play a crucial role in demonstrating the value that Fizen brings.
