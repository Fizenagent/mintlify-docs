# Users & Community

Fizen's goal isn't to serve the small group of people already familiar with crypto. It's to reach the **billions who haven't been exposed to it yet**.

Making crypto accessible to everyday users — people who've never touched a wallet, never heard of a blockchain — is the real challenge. And it requires more than just a good product.

## How we grow the community

- **Events and campaigns** — introducing crypto to the public in a gentle, understandable way
- **KOC programs** — key opinion customers sharing real experiences, not paid promotions
- **Partner ecosystems** — brands that bring their own audiences into the Fizen network
- **Referral program** — users earning commission for every friend they bring on

## The journey since 2022

Fizen started in Vietnam and Southeast Asia — markets where QR payments are already part of daily life, and where crypto adoption is growing fast.

From day one, the focus was on **real usage**, not just downloads. Users who spend USDT on Fizen. Merchants who accept it. A community that grows because the product actually works.
