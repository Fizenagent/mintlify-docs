The global logistics and transportation industry is rapidly evolving. It has seen the lowest cargo transportation costs ever, creating significant opportunities for cross-border e-commerce.

The model of selling through KOC (Key Opinion Customers) and social media platforms has been very successful in China and South Korea for many years. We have an advantage in our relationships with global goods suppliers. We also have experience, knowledge, and relationships in the entertainment industry in South Korea and China, especially with professional sales KOC. Fizen will leverage these strengths to help businesses sell globally, and customers to purchase products at lower costs
