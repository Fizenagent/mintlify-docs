Surely, all of us have experienced the inconvenience of having to exchange currency or use costly cards while traveling. Fizen is here to make your travel experience better, more comfortable, and convenient.

Currently, we are focusing on pilot testing for both Vietnamese travelers going abroad and foreign tourists coming to Vietnam. This app is not just for tourists but also for people living and working abroad, especially those who are freelancers working online, earning income from foreign sources, and living a "digital nomad" lifestyle. They often face difficulties opening a bank account due to a lack of employment contracts and encounter challenges when transferring money to and from their home countries. Their primary option is to deal with cash.

While other projects might aim to bring cryptocurrency "to the moon," Fizen's mission is to bring cryptocurrency "down to earth" by offering practical and highly useful applications. These applications range from facilitating payments for a private jet flight to something as simple as paying for a bowl of noodle. The benefits of this application are substantial, delivering a superior travel experience for tourists and boosting revenue of the tourism industry.
