# Business Scalability

Fizen doesn't directly sell products or provide services. Everything is delivered by our partners.

This is a deliberate design choice — and it's what makes Fizen scalable.

---

## The platform model

Fizen builds the infrastructure. Partners plug in and serve users.

- A merchant integrates **Fizen Pay** → instantly gains access to Fizen's user base
- Fizen users gain a new place to spend their USDT
- The ecosystem grows for everyone

This is the same model behind the world's largest platforms:

> YouTube doesn't create content. Airbnb doesn't own hotels. Uber doesn't own cars.
> **Fizen doesn't sell products — it builds the platform that connects buyers and sellers.**

---

## Growth curve

In the early stage, growth is linear — one partner, one integration at a time.

As the number of partners grows, the value of the network compounds. More merchants → more useful for users → more users → more valuable for merchants.

**Linear → Exponential.**

---

## Global by design

Fizen is built on USDT — a globally used stablecoin. This means Fizen's ecosystem isn't limited to one country or region.

Every new market that adopts USDT is a potential market for Fizen.
