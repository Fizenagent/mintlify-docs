# Welcome to Fizen

### **Fizen is the crypto neobank built for travelers and global citizens.**

Fizen simplifies life on the move. Whether you’re exploring Southeast Asia, living abroad, or working re, Fizen helps you pay like a local, travel smoothly, send money across borders, and manage your crypto - all in one simple app.

Beyond everyday payments, Fizen is designed to make better use of your crypto. While you travel, spend, or live globally, your idle balance can stay productive in the background, helping you build long-term value without adding complexity to your daily life.

### What you can do with Fizen

* Pay like a local with crypto using QR scan or cards, especially convenient across SEA and LATAM
* Spend globally with one card linked to one crypto balance
* Travel with ease - book hotels, flights, and stay connected with global eSIM
* Send money internationally with fast crypto transfers across borders
* Buy and sell crypto using bank transfers and cards
* Access tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs directly from your crypto balance
* Manage your crypto securely in one place
* Put idle money to work in the background, not just spend it

Built for life on the move

Fizen is designed for people who live, work, and travel across regions - from the US and Europe to Southeast Asia and LATAM. It’s especially useful in SEA, where QR payments and local digital payments are part of everyday life.
