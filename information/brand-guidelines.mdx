| **Fizen Cyan** | `#46E0E1` | Accent — secondary highlights |

| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark backgrounds |
| **Dark Navy** | `#323A4C` | Body text, dark elements |
| **Black** | `#000000` | Headlines on light backgrounds |

## Typography

**Font:** Inter — all weights, all sizes.

---

## Logo

- Don't stretch, rotate, or recolor it
- Use white version on dark backgrounds, dark version on light
- Keep clear space around it — don't crowd it with other elements

---

## Voice

**Direct.** Say what you mean. Skip the filler.

**Trustworthy.** Back claims with facts. No vague promises.

**Simple.** If a non-crypto person can't understand it, rewrite it.

**No hype.** Specific numbers beat adjectives. "150M+ merchants" beats "massive global network."
