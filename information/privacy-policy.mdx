## Cookies

Like most websites and applications, we use cookies and similar technologies (e.g. web beacons, flash cookies) ("Cookies") to automatically collect information — such as which web pages you click on and how you use the Fizen Platform. We use this data to measure and analyse usage, enhance your experience, improve our Services, and provide personalised suggestions across your devices.

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device by websites and applications. They help sites remember you, your login details, and other preferences. By using the Fizen Platform and accepting this Policy, you consent to our use of Cookies.

Third parties we engage may also use Cookies or similar technologies to collect information automatically. We are not responsible for those third parties' privacy practices, and their data practices are not covered by this Policy. If you choose to use third-party platforms or services, please review their applicable privacy policies.

You may refuse or disable Cookies by adjusting your browser settings. However, doing so may limit the functionality of the Fizen Platform and some Services may no longer be available to you. Because each browser handles this differently, please consult your browser's instructions for guidance. Note that opt-out settings are browser and device-specific, you may need to opt out separately for each.
