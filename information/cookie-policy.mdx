## 2. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

A "cookie" is a small text file that a website places on your device when you visit it. Cookies are widely used to make websites work or work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the operators of the site.

Similar technologies covered by this Policy include:

- **Pixels** (also called web beacons) — small graphical files that can be embedded in a web page or email to track interactions.
- **Software Development Kits (SDKs)** — pieces of code in mobile apps that perform functions similar to cookies on websites.
- **Local storage** — data stored by the browser or app on your device (HTML5 local storage, IndexedDB, app storage).
- **Device identifiers** — identifiers assigned to your device (advertising IDs such as IDFA on iOS or AAID on Android).
- **Browser fingerprinting** — techniques that identify your device based on a combination of characteristics.

References to "cookies" in this Policy include all of these technologies, except where the context indicates otherwise.

## 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the Platform to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions made by you that amount to a request for service, such as logging in, completing a form, or persisting your consent choices. They include:

- **Authentication** — cookies that keep you signed in to your Fizen account, identify your session, and protect against session hijacking.
- **Security** — cookies that detect fraud, support multi-factor authentication, store CSRF tokens and similar.
- **Load balancing and routing** — cookies that direct your traffic to the correct server.
- **Consent storage** — cookies that record your choices in the cookie banner and preferences centre.
- **Functionality required to deliver Services** — cookies that are necessary to operate the core Fizen Wallet, Card, QR Pay, Pay, Marketplace, Swap and Rewards Program features.

These cookies do not require your consent under Applicable Privacy Law because they are strictly necessary for a service you have explicitly requested.

### 3.2 Functional cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation, such as remembering your language, currency, dark-mode preference, recently viewed Marketplace Items, and similar. If you do not allow these cookies, some functionality will be unavailable or less smooth.

### 3.3 Analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors and users interact with the Platform so we can measure performance, identify popular content, and improve the Platform. They include first-party analytics cookies and cookies from third-party analytics providers identified in the inventory in clause 6.

### 3.4 Marketing and advertising cookies

These cookies and pixels are set by Fizen and by our advertising partners to deliver advertising that is more relevant to your interests, to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, and to support attribution. They include cookies that allow us to build a profile of your interests and show relevant adverts on other sites. They may share information with third-party advertising platforms (including, where you have consented, Meta, Google, TikTok, X, and similar). These cookies may constitute a "sale" or "share" under US state privacy laws — see clause 4.3.

## 4. Consent and Your Choices

### 4.1 How we obtain consent

When you first visit the Platform, we display a cookie consent banner. The banner allows you to (a) accept all cookies, (b) reject all non-essential cookies, with the same prominence as "accept", or (c) customise your choices for each category. Your choices are stored and you can change them at any time through the cookie preferences centre accessible from the Platform. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving consent.

### 4.2 EEA, UK and other opt-in regimes

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or another jurisdiction that requires prior consent for non-essential cookies, we will set such cookies only after you have indicated your consent through the banner or preferences centre. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

### 4.3 California and other US state privacy laws

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and similar US state laws (Virginia VCDPA, Colorado CPA, Connecticut CTDPA, Utah UCPA, Texas TDPSA, Oregon OCPA, Montana CDPA and others), our use of marketing and advertising cookies may constitute a "sale" or "share" of personal information. You may opt out at any time by:

- clicking the **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link in the footer of the Platform (where displayed);
- configuring your cookie preferences in the cookie banner or preferences centre to reject marketing and advertising cookies; or
- sending a **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal from your browser; we respect GPC as a valid opt-out.

### 4.4 Browser controls

Most browsers let you control cookies through their settings (block all, block third-party, delete on exit, do-not-track, and similar). You can also use private/incognito mode. Doing so may limit Platform functionality, including signing in to your account.

### 4.5 Mobile device controls

On iOS, you can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can also disable cross-app tracking through the App Tracking Transparency framework on iOS.

### 4.6 Industry opt-outs

You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many third-party advertising partners through industry tools, including:

- YourAdChoices (Digital Advertising Alliance, US) — [https://youradchoices.com/](https://youradchoices.com/)
- European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA) — [https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)
- Network Advertising Initiative — [https://thenai.org/opt-out/](https://thenai.org/opt-out/)

### 4.7 Browser "Do Not Track"

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is no industry standard for how DNT should be interpreted, and we do not currently respond to DNT signals (other than as required by Applicable Law). We do, however, respect the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as set out above.
