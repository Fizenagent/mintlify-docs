---
title: "Voting Gateway"
---

Fizen's Voting Gateway enables projects and communities to run on-chain governance votes using their token — with results displayed and executed transparently.

---

## Use cases

- **Token governance** — let holders vote on protocol changes, treasury allocation, or product direction
- **Community decisions** — polls and proposals with verifiable on-chain results
- **Partnership votes** — let your community weigh in on major partnerships or integrations

---

## How it works

1. Project submits a proposal with voting options and deadline
2. Token holders vote using their Fizen wallet
3. Results are recorded on-chain — transparent and immutable
4. Outcome is executed based on pre-defined rules
