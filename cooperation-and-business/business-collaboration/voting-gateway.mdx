In addition to blockchain-related products, Fizen also owns a voting gateway and software development system for various programs and competitions. Fizen provides modern, secure, and highly interactive technology solutions for online voting gates, both locally and internationally.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Voting 1.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

With experience in major competitions such as Miss Charm 2022 and Miss Universe Vietnam 2023, Fizen is a trusted partner for competition organizers, event planners, and television programs. Using an automatic scaling and load balancing system to ensure availability and consistency even during peak times, along with the ability to accept various payment methods to maximize the user experience, Fizen's voting system provides the smoothest user experience ever.

Fizen not only provides technology solutions; we accompany the organizing committee in their communication activities, create diverse experiences, and contribute to boosting community engagement.
