title: "Integrating Tokens to Fizen App"
---

Add your token to the Fizen Super App so users can hold, send, and spend it — alongside USDT and other supported assets.

---

## What integration gives your token

- **Visibility** — appears in Fizen's wallet for hundreds of thousands of users
- **Spendability** — users can pay with your token at Fizen Pay merchants
- **Liquidity** — accessible via Fizen's P2P and swap features
- **Trust** — listed alongside verified, audited assets

---

## How to apply

Contact [business@fizen.io](mailto:business@fizen.io) with:
- Token name, symbol, and contract address
- Blockchain network
- Project overview and website
- Any existing community or exchange listings

Our team will review and respond within 5–7 business days.
