title: "Integrate Stablecoin"
---

Add stablecoin support to Fizen's payment infrastructure — enabling your stablecoin to be used for real-world spending via Fizen Card and QR Pay.

---

## What this means

Your stablecoin becomes:
- **Loadable** onto Fizen Card for Visa spending at 150M+ merchants
- **Usable** for QR Pay at merchants across SEA and beyond
- **Transferable** via Fizen's wallet to any compatible address

---

## Requirements

- Audited smart contract
- Stable peg mechanism
- Active liquidity on at least one major DEX or exchange
- Legal compliance documentation
